## Full voiceover (141 words)

> In October 2026, Google launched AI chips into space, to test whether data centres could orbit Earth.
> Why? On Earth, AI is running short of grid connections, land and water. In the right orbit, solar panels almost never go dark. Google says they can produce up to eight times more power.
> Starcloud has already trained a small AI model on an Nvidia chip in orbit. SpaceX has asked to launch up to a million computing satellites. Only about 16,800 working satellites exist today.
> The catch: there's no air to carry heat away. Chips must radiate it through huge panels, around 100 tonnes per megawatt by one estimate. Add radiation, and no repairs.
> Google itself says it might add up by the mid-2030s.
> So it's an experiment, not an escape hatch. AI's clean power still has to be built on the ground.

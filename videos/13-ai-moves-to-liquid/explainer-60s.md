## Full voiceover (151 words)

> The newest AI chips run so hot that fans can't cool them. So data centres are turning into plumbing.
> A normal server rack draws about 11 kilowatts. Nvidia's newest AI rack packs 72 chips tightly together so they work as one giant chip. It draws about 120.
> Every watt becomes heat, and air can only carry away about 20 kilowatts a rack.
> Litre for litre, water holds about 3,500 times more heat than air. So cold plates now sit right on the chips, or whole servers sink into a fluid that doesn't conduct electricity.
> Does that mean more water? Not if the loop is closed. Microsoft says its zero-evaporation design saves 125 million litres a year per site.
> In hot, humid India, operators like Yotta already run their AI chips on liquid.
> So next time you hear 'AI data centre', ask: how is it cooled, and where does the heat go?

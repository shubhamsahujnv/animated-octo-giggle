## LinkedIn

One AI server rack now draws more than 10× the power of a typical rack. That changes how data centres are cooled.

→ The most common rack in the industry runs at about 11 kW (Uptime Institute, 2026).
→ An NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 AI rack draws about 120 kW, and NVIDIA's roadmap points to ~600 kW racks in 2027.
→ Air cooling reaches its practical limit at roughly 20 kW per rack.
→ Per litre, water holds ~3,500× more heat than air, so operators are moving to direct-to-chip and immersion cooling. In India, operators such as Yotta say their GPU loads already run on liquid.
→ Liquid can save water if the loop is closed. Microsoft estimates its zero-evaporation design saves 125 million litres a year per site, with first sites from late 2027.

The caveat: if that heat still leaves through evaporating cooling towers, water use comes back. Cooling design, not just the cooling medium, decides the footprint.

Which should India's new AI data centres be judged on first: energy efficiency or water use?

Sources: Uptime Institute, Global Data Center Survey (2026); NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 (2024) and Rubin Ultra roadmap (reported); Yotta Data Services (2026); Microsoft (Aug 2024, company estimate).

First, the basics

So, what is
a carbon credit?

A tradable certificate for one tonne of CO₂e that a project avoided or removed, checked against a published standard.

Issued
by a third-party standard
Traded
through a marketplace
Retired
once claimed, never resold
Source: Verra VCS and Gold Standard programme rules (unit definitions).

For your team

Four questions
to ask the seller.

Which standard issued it?
Ask for the registry and serial number.
Is it additional?
Would the cut have happened without it?
How long does it last?
Check how reversal risk is handled.
Can you see it retired?
In your company's name, on the public registry.
03 / 05
The honest part

Credits help.
They are not a substitute
for cutting emissions.

Reduce what you can first. Use credits for what you can't yet.
Please note: Carbon credits do not eliminate emissions and should be used as part of a broader decarbonization strategy.

## LinkedIn
Not every carbon credit carries the same weight. Before procurement signs off, four questions separate a credible credit from a liability:

→ Which standard issued it, and what is the registry serial number?
→ Is it additional: would the reduction have happened anyway?
→ How is permanence and reversal risk handled?
→ Can you see it retired in your company's name on the public registry?

### H3 — What does an AI development company actually do?

We take an AI idea that works in a demo and make it survive production: retrieval that holds up
on real documents, guardrails that stop the model doing damage, evaluation that tells you when
quality drops, and the cloud architecture underneath. The demo is the easy part.

### H3 — How do I choose a custom software development company?

Ask for the names and CVs of the engineers who will actually be on your project, not the agency
average. Ask who owns the code and IP. Ask what happens at handover. Full detail:
[how to choose a software development company](/blog/how-to-choose-software-development-company).

### H3 — Do we own the code and the IP?

Yes, entirely, including during the engagement. There is no license-back, no retained component
and no hosting lock-in.

### H3 — What does custom software development cost?

It depends on scope, but we give you a cost envelope at the end of discovery rather than after
you have committed. For benchmarks, see
[mobile app development cost, USA and Australia](/blog/mobile-app-development-cost-usa-australia)
and [in India](/blog/mobile-app-development-cost-india).

### H3 — How quickly can you start?

Specialist augmentation typically within two weeks. A full squad in three to four.

### H3 — Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes — funded startups. [Where to build your MVP](/blog/best-mvp-development-companies-india) is
a good read first if you are at that stage.

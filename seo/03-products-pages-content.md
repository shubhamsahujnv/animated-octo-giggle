# Products — Optimised Content

The current `/products` page has a structural fault: its H1 is "AI voice agents that sound human
and get real work done", which is PXO AI's H1, on a page that also lists EchoMind and AI Device
Inspector. One URL cannot rank for an AI voice platform, a journalling app and a device
inspection tool — the topics have nothing in common semantically.

The fix is a hub-and-spoke split: `/products` becomes a hub targeting the AI agent development
terms, and each product gets its own page owning its own cluster.

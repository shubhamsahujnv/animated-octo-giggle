| `/locations/india` | ai development company in india | 210 | 28 |

Australia at SD 6 is essentially free, and four Australia blog posts already exist to support it.

**One caution.** The firm is remote-first with no stated office address. Location pages for
places you have no presence in are thin-content risk and can read as deceptive. Write them as
*market* pages — who we serve there, time-zone overlap, currency, contracting, compliance,
client work in that market — not as fake office pages. Do not add `LocalBusiness` schema or
invent addresses.

clearly on the page. A comparison that hides its own interest tends to get treated as thin
affiliate content, and it damages trust with the buyers who notice.

## Pages I would not build

Worth saying explicitly, because agencies often sell these:

- **City pages** (`/locations/usa/dallas`, etc.). `mobile app development company in dallas` has
  1,600 volume and looks tempting, but with no Dallas presence these are thin doorway pages. The
  risk outweighs the return at DA 6.
- **A page per technology** (`/react-native`, `/flutter`, `/kubernetes`). These cannibalise the
  service pages. Cover technologies as sections within the relevant service page.
- **Separate pages for each academic process term** (v-model, waterfall, SDLC phases). Student
  traffic that will not convert, and it dilutes the commercial signal on `/process`.

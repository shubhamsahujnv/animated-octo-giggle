# Copy and claims

Applies to everything on an image, in a video, and in its caption or share copy.

## Voice

Professional, transparent, fact-based, hopeful. The arc is **problem → evidence → solution**, with an honest caveat where one is due. Audience: sustainability managers, auditors, consultants and informed professionals in India. Write for someone who will check the numbers.

Brag's "funny earns its place" is allowed only as dry wit drawn from a real fact (a surprising comparison, a deadpan understatement). Never jokes about climate harm, never parody of real companies or people, never mockery of the audience.

## Banned (the checker fails on these)

| Phrase | Why |
|---|---|
| TGS-verified, TGS certified | TGS is a marketplace facilitator; third-party standards certify credits |
| guaranteed, guarantee (about outcomes, offsets or returns) | Unsupported guarantee |
| 100% carbon neutral, completely offset, erase your footprint, cancel out your emissions | Greenwashing: credits do not eliminate emissions |
| revolutionary, game-changing, supercharge, unlock, elevate, streamline your workflow, excited to share | Hype / generic SaaS language (TGS voice + brag ban list) |

## Flagged for review (the checker warns)

`carbon neutral`, `net zero`, `eco-friendly`, `green` used as a claim, `sustainable` used as a claim, `offset` used as a verb about a company's footprint. Each needs a qualifier and a source on the same slide/scene, or rewording.

## Numbers

- Every number on a slide or scene has a **source line** on that same slide/scene: `Source: Publisher, report (year).`
- Verify each figure before use. If reports disagree, name the measure you used. Cut any figure you cannot stand behind.
- Charts and bars are proportional to the real values; if illustrative, say so in the source line.
- Captions and share copy may not contain any number that isn't on the slides/scenes.
- In video, brag's "free to invent" covers tone, framing, hooks and transitions only. Names, numbers, capabilities and quotes must be grounded.

## Carbon credits: required disclosures

Whenever carbon credits are mentioned anywhere in the piece (slides, scenes, caption, share copy), both lines must appear:

1. Carbon credits do not eliminate emissions and should be used as part of a broader decarbonization strategy.
2. Credits listed on this platform are issued and certified by third-party standards. The Green Solve acts as a marketplace facilitator.

Where: on images, as the source line (or a `.note`) of the slide that mentions credits, plus the closing slide. In video, in the source band of each scene that mentions credits plus a closing disclosure card (timing in `scale.md` §6). In captions/share copy, in full at the end.

## Privacy

Never show real customer names, emails, project IDs, internal URLs or credentials. If real product UI shows such data, swap in plausible fictional stand-ins and note it in the plan.

## Captions

- **Instagram:** hook line + emoji, 3–5 short lines or ✅ points, a question CTA, 8–12 hashtags including #TheGreenSolve.
- **LinkedIn:** professional, data-forward, bullet/arrow list, a discussion question, a sources line, 3–5 hashtags.
- **Video share copy (`share-copy.txt`):** 1–3 sentences, postable as-is, no "excited to share", plus the disclosures when credits are mentioned.

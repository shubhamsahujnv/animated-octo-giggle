## 5. Off-page SEO and backlink plan

**Rules:** no PBNs, paid link packages, or spun guest posts. Google penalises these, and in a trust-heavy niche (visas, company formation) they also hurt reputation. Keep NAP (name, address, phone **04 244 3290**) identical everywhere.

### Tier 1: Local foundation and citations (Weeks 1–3, ~35 links)

| Type | Targets |
|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | Category "Business management consultant" / "Corporate office"; all 8 services added as Services; weekly posts; photos of the office and team; Q&A seeded from the FAQs |
| Maps / big platforms | Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Waze, LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase |
| UAE business directories | Yellow Pages UAE, Connect.ae, Dubai Chamber member directory, 2GIS Dubai, Hidubai (by Dubai Economy), Etisalat Yellow Pages, Dubizzle Services, ServiceMarket, UAE Business Directory, Zawya company profile |
| Expat / relocation | ExpatWoman directory, Expat.com, InterNations (partner listing), Dubai expat guides |
| Review platforms | Google reviews (**target 30 in 90 days**), Trustpilot, Clutch (business services) |

### Tier 2: Industry-authority links (Weeks 2–8, ~20 links). This is the biggest lever.

1. **Free zone authorised-partner listings.** Most free zones list their registered business-setup partners/agents on their official sites. These are high-DA, perfectly relevant links. Apply as an agent/partner with: **IFZA, Meydan Free Zone, DMCC, RAKEZ, SPC Free Zone, Shams, Ajman Free Zone, DWC, DAFZA, DDA**. The competitors get a large share of their authority this way.
2. **Business councils and chambers:** Dubai Chamber, British Business Group, Indian Business & Professional Council (IBPC), German Emirati Joint Council, French Business Council, Pakistan/Filipino business councils. Membership usually includes a member-directory link plus event speaking slots.
3. **Partner pages:** banks' SME referral programmes, accounting/VAT firms, coworking and business centres, PRO typing centres, translation/attestation agencies, insurance brokers. Swap "trusted partners" pages and co-written guides.
4. **Embassy / trade-office supplier lists:** some trade promotion bodies (UK DBT, India's consulate commercial wing, EU chambers) list service providers for companies entering the UAE.

### Tier 3: Content-led and PR links (Weeks 6–12, ~15 high-DR links)

1. **Linkable assets [Proposal: client to approve as Insights additions]:**
   - **Dubai Free Zone Cost Comparison 2027:** a table of licence fee, visa quota and office options across ~15 free zones, updated quarterly. Bloggers, expat forums and journalists link to this kind of resource.
   - **Golden Visa eligibility checker:** a simple interactive (salary, investment, profession) linking to the Golden Visa page.
   - **Mainland vs free zone vs offshore decision table:** already approved, so pitch it to expat and startup sites as the reference.
2. **Digital PR / expert commentary:** position a named Businessco expert (needs the Our Team page). Pitch comment on corporate tax updates, visa rule changes and free zone news to **Gulf News, Khaleej Times, The National, Arabian Business, Gulf Business, Entrepreneur Middle East, Zawya, Wamda, Magnitt, Time Out Dubai (business)**. Use Qwoted / Featured.com / Help a B2B Writer for international queries on "moving business to Dubai".
3. **Guest articles:** 2 per month on accounting, fintech, startup, relocation and expat blogs (UAE and source markets: India, UK, Pakistan, Europe). Use one contextual link to a service page.
4. **Press releases (sparingly):** launch announcement on Zawya / WAM-style distribution. Use them for brand mentions, not link value.
5. **Unlinked mentions + broken links:** monitor "Businessco" mentions monthly. Find dead links on "Dubai business setup guide" resource pages and offer the matching Insights article.

### Anchor-text mix
Branded (Businessco, businessco.ae) **50%** · URL **15%** · Generic **15%** · Partial match (e.g. "free zone company setup in Dubai") **15%** · Exact match **≤5%**.

- Answer questions on **Reddit (r/dubai, r/UAE, r/expats)** and **Quora** from a named expert account. Be genuinely helpful, and link only when relevant.
- Keep the 40–60 word opening + FAQ format on every service page (already in the structure doc).

## 7. Social media plan

The structure doc confirms **LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram** and a **WhatsApp** chat. Add **Google Business Profile posts** (free, local SEO). **[Proposal]** add YouTube/TikTok in phase 2 for short explainer videos.

### Channel roles and cadence
| Channel | Audience | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (company + 1–2 named experts) | Founders, foreign companies, SME owners, HR/finance | Authority, B2B leads | Company 4×/wk, experts 2×/wk each |
| **Instagram** (Reels + carousels) | Entrepreneurs, freelancers, expats, Indian/Pakistani/European founders | Reach, simple explainers | 4–5×/wk + daily Stories |
| **X / Twitter** | Media, business community | UAE business news commentary, rule updates | 1×/day |
| **Google Business Profile** | Local searchers | Offers, updates, FAQs | 1–2×/wk |
| **WhatsApp** (Business catalogue + channel) | Leads and clients | Fast replies, catalogue of the 8 services, update broadcasts | Replies < 15 min in working hours |

### Content pillars
1. **Setup explained (35%):** "Mainland vs free zone in 30 seconds", "3 documents you need for a DMCC licence", cost breakdown carousels. Each piece links to the matching service or Insights page.
2. **Visa and residency (20%):** Golden Visa eligibility myths, family visa steps, residence visa timelines.
3. **Rule updates / news (15%):** corporate tax deadlines, new free zone packages, visa rule changes. Post fast; this earns shares and press attention.
4. **Trust and proof (20%):** client success stories (with permission), Google review highlights, team faces, behind-the-scenes at government offices and free zones, "a day with our PRO".
5. **Why the UAE / Why Dubai (10%):** lifestyle and business advantages for founders in India, Pakistan, the UK and Europe.

Quick answers

Journal FAQs

What topics does The Baseline Journal cover?+

Carbon accounting under ISO 14064-1 and the GHG Protocol, Scope 1, 2 and 3 measurement, carbon credit eligibility and markets, and reporting rules in India and the United States, written for small and mid-sized manufacturers.

Who writes for the journal?+

The TGS editorial desk, TheGreensolve founder Ankita Patwa and invited contributors such as plant managers, sustainability leads, auditors, financiers and founders who have done the work they write about.

How can I contribute an article?+

Use the Write with us form on the blog page to send your name, company, working title and argument to our editors, then attach your draft. Contributions are invite-based, and pitches of 800 to 1,200 words with one clear argument work best.

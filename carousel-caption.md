## Caption — primary

You ranked #1. And nobody saw it.

Half your buyers now get their answer before a single blue link loads — in
ChatGPT, in an AI Overview, in a snippet they never clicked.

Ranking is one surface. There are five:

→ SEO gets you FOUND — ranked on the results page
→ GEO gets you REFERENCED — named and cited inside AI answers
→ AEO gets you ANSWERED — your words lifted into the snippet
→ AIO gets you UNDERSTOOD — trusted as a source worth summarising
→ SXO gets you CHOSEN — the click turns into a customer

These aren't competing strategies. They're five checkpoints on one journey,
and a gap in any one of them leaks the work done by the rest.

If you're starting from zero, don't do all five at once. Fix the experience,
make the content extractable, build the ranking base, earn the summary, then
chase the mention. Each step makes the next one cheaper.

Swipe through the five → and tell me: which one is your business quietly
ignoring in 2026?

## First comment (seed the thread)

Quick breakdown of the sequence we run, in order:

1. SXO — speed, mobile, intent match. Traffic sent to a slow page is money burned.
2. AEO — question-led headings + schema. One pass feeds snippets, AI answers and voice.
3. SEO — technical health, depth, links. Still the biggest single channel.
4. AIO — experience signals and topical coverage, so AI Overviews quote you.
5. GEO — off-site presence and original data, measured as share of citations.

Happy to go deeper on any one of them.

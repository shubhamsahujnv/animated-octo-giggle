## 1. ⚠️ Read this before investing in the page's SEO

The page is hosted at **`lynkk-revenue-story.imsjwagh99.chatgpt.site`** — a subdomain of a
shared third-party domain. For SEO purposes that has three consequences:

1. **No brand equity accrues to Lynkk.** Any authority this page earns attaches to
   `chatgpt.site`, not to you. If the page moves later, you start from zero again.
2. **You don't control the root domain** — robots.txt, sitemap registration, canonical policy
   and the neighbourhood of other subdomains are all outside your hands. The missing sitemap
   (the single audit error) may not even be fixable from where the page sits.
3. **Shared-domain subdomains are discounted.** Search engines treat a free-host subdomain as a
   weaker signal than a brand domain, so the keyword targets below will underperform what their
   difficulty scores suggest.

**Recommendation:** treat this URL as a preview or stakeholder draft. Ship the final copy on
**`lynkk.ai`** — as the homepage, or at `lynkk.ai/sales`. That domain is weak (DA 1, 3 referring
domains, 4 nofollow backlinks — see `03-expanded-keywords.md` §1) but it is *yours*, and every
link it earns compounds.

This doesn't change the copy, so it isn't a reason to wait. Just don't buy links or run a
content programme pointing at a `chatgpt.site` URL.

## 3. Recommended page architecture

The title *"Your revenue team's memory"* points at teams; Clusters A and B are individual-rep
searches. Both are viable — but one page cannot rank for both well. Suggested split:

| Page | Primary target | Vol / SD | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homepage** | ai meeting notes *(not `note taker` — see `06` §4)* | 1,000 / 41 | Both — brand + category |
| `/follow-up-email-after-sales-call` | follow up email to sales call | **8,100 / 7** | Rep |
| `/discovery-call` | discovery call | 4,400 / 25 | Rep |
| `/discovery-call-template` | discovery call template | 50 / **14** | Rep — lead magnet |
| `/for-revenue-teams` | conversation intelligence platform | 880 / 37 | Buying committee |
| `/chrome-extension` *(copy in `07`)* | how to record a meeting on zoom | **9,900 / 13** | Rep — converts to install |
| `/zoom-meeting-recorder` → fold into `/chrome-extension` | zoom meeting recorder | 2,900 / **17** | Rep — converts to extension |

**Build order, revised after pass 5:** the **`/chrome-extension` page first** — `how to record a
meeting on zoom` is 9,900/mo at **SD 13**, the best ratio found anywhere in this engagement, and
it converts straight to an install. Then the follow-up page (8,100 / SD 7), then discovery call,
then the template as a lead magnet linking to both. Homepage head terms last — they need
authority the domain doesn't have yet.

Fold the planned `/zoom-meeting-recorder` page into `/chrome-extension` rather than building both;
they'd compete for the same cluster.

## 3. Description

```
THE CONVERSATION MEMORY PLATFORM FOR REVENUE TEAMS

Lynkk is a conversation memory platform. It records your calls, writes the
notes and the action items, and connects what was said across every
conversation — so your team can ask what a customer told you in March and
get an answer, not a video file.

A note taker app gives you one document per meeting. Lynkk gives you a
memory: commitments, decisions and people linked across weeks and months,
and queryable in plain language.

Works on Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams. No bot joins your call.
No screen capture. One click.


WHY A MEMORY BEATS A PILE OF NOTES

Notes go stale the moment the meeting ends. Nobody re-reads a summary from
six weeks ago, and nobody rewatches a 47-minute recording to find one
sentence about pricing.

A memory works differently. Every call Lynkk records joins the same
connected record, so:

• Ask a question, get an answer — "What did Acme say about budget?" "Who
  signs off on this?" "What did we promise in the Q2 review?" — answered
  from the actual conversation, with the source linked.
• Context survives people — when a rep leaves or an account changes hands,
  the history goes with the account, not out the door.
• Commitments stop slipping — every promise made on a call is captured with
  an owner and a date, linked back to the moment it was made.
• Nothing has to be rebuilt — no re-reading old notes before a renewal
  call, no asking a colleague what happened last quarter.


NOTHING JOINS YOUR MEETING

Most meeting tools dial into the call as a visible participant. Your
prospect watches "Notetaker" ask to be admitted, and the first minute of a
discovery call goes on explaining a robot instead of building rapport.

Lynkk records from your own browser tab. No extra participant, nothing to
admit, nothing to apologise for.

Your screen is never captured — audio only.
No tab other than the one you pressed Record on is ever touched.
Call audio and your microphone are recorded on separate channels, so speech
is attributed to the right person — useful when you need to know whether an
objection came from your champion or their CFO.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Install Lynkk and sign in at lynkk.ai. No second login, no setup screens.
2. Join your call — Google Meet, Zoom in the browser, or Microsoft Teams.
3. Press Record. Lynkk detects the call and starts capturing.
4. Hang up. The summary, action items and the call itself join your memory.


WHAT EVERY CALL ADDS TO YOUR MEMORY

• A summary — what was discussed, decided and left open
• Action items — each commitment with an owner and a date
• An answerable record — ask anything about the call and get a cited answer
• Connections — decisions and people linked to every related conversation
• Searchable history — find that pricing objection from three weeks ago
• Notes ready to paste into your CRM or your follow-up email


WORKS WHERE YOU WORK

• Google Meet — records the call, adds it to your memory, nothing to admit
• Zoom — works with calls you join in the browser
• Microsoft Teams — client calls and internal ones alike


FOR YOUR ROLE

Account Executives — run the discovery call instead of typing through it,
then walk into the next one knowing exactly what was promised in the last.

SDRs and BDRs — more conversations a day. Qualification notes and next
steps write themselves, so you move to the next dial, not to a notes doc.

Account Managers and Customer Success — renewals and QBRs stay on record.
What the customer asked for last quarter is one question away.

Sales Managers — coach from what was actually said, not from what got
typed up afterwards.

Founders selling their own product — every early customer call becomes
product input you can go back and read, not a feeling you half-remember.


ONE CALL, OR A YEAR OF THEM

Install Lynkk for today's meeting and you get a good set of notes.

Keep it running for a month and you have something a note taker app can't
give you: a memory of the whole account — every commitment, every decision,
every objection, connected and answerable.


PRIVACY AND CONSENT

Your screen is never captured. Lynkk records audio only.
No tab other than the one you pressed Record on is ever touched.
Nothing joins your meeting as a participant.

Recording laws differ by country and state, and many require everyone's
consent. Tell the people on the call that you are recording. Good practice —
and in a sales conversation it builds more trust than it costs.


PRICING

Free to start — the free plan is enough to use this extension.


GET STARTED

Install Lynkk, sign in at lynkk.ai, and press Record on your next call.


FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is a conversation memory platform?
A note taker app records one meeting and gives you one document. A
conversation memory platform connects every meeting — the commitments,
decisions and people — into a single record you can ask questions of. The
notes are the input; the memory is the product.

Is this a note taker app?
Lynkk does everything a note taker app does — records the call, writes the
summary, extracts the action items. It then keeps all of it connected and
queryable, which is the part a note taker app stops short of.

Is there a note taker that doesn't join the meeting?
That's what this is. Lynkk records from your browser, so no bot appears in
the participant list and nobody has to admit one.

How do I record a meeting on Zoom?
Join the meeting in a browser tab and press Record. You don't need to be
the host or on a paid Zoom plan. Tell the others you're recording.

Does Lynkk work with Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?
Yes, both. Press Record in the call tab.

Will my prospect know I'm recording?
Not from a bot in the participant list — there isn't one. You should still
tell them. Many jurisdictions require it, and it's the right way to run a
call.

Does it record my screen or my other tabs?
No. Audio only, and only the tab you pressed Record on.

Can I ask questions about past calls?
Yes. Ask across your whole call history in plain language and get an answer
with the source conversation linked.

Do I need a Lynkk account?
Yes. Sign in at lynkk.ai — the extension follows that session, so there's
no separate login here.

Is there a free plan?
Yes. The free plan is enough to use this extension.
```

## 6. Confirm before publishing

Four claims in the copy depend on product facts worth checking first.

| Claim in the copy | Confirm |
|---|---|
| "Context survives people" and the Sales Managers line | That memory is genuinely **shared across a team**, not per-user. This is the listing's load-bearing claim — if memory is per-user, both lines need rewording. |
| "a memory of the whole account" | Any **retention limit** on the free or paid plan. The memory framing implies indefinite recall. |
| "Notes ready to paste into your CRM" | Keep as-is unless a real CRM integration exists. Do not upgrade to "syncs with your CRM" without one. |
| "Free to start" | The free plan's actual limit. Stating a number ("5 calls a month") converts better than "free to start", and the wording must match lynkk.ai exactly — a mismatch drives uninstalls, and uninstall rate affects store ranking. |

**One item to resolve before submitting.** The listing states that nothing joins your meeting.
The page `lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot` is currently the only page Google has indexed for the
domain, and it ranks for "zoom meeting bot". A store reviewer who finds it may read this listing
as a deceptive claim. Either retire or redirect that page, or label both pages explicitly by
product.

Optional additions once documented: encryption and GDPR compliance (only with a DPA and privacy
policy behind them, and name the retention period), and whether a full transcript is surfaced to
the user.

## 7. What to expect

This listing leads with the conversation memory positioning rather than a search-led category
term. That keeps it consistent with lynkk.ai and the wider product story, and it is written to
convert visitors who arrive already knowing what Lynkk is — from the website, outbound, or a
referral.

Store-search discovery will stay modest as a result, and store ranking is driven mainly by
install count, review volume and uninstall rate rather than by copy. The two highest-impact
actions alongside this listing are a dedicated Chrome extension page on lynkk.ai, which can
capture the substantial organic demand around recording Zoom and Google Meet calls, and a push
to roughly 25 installs with 5 reviews.

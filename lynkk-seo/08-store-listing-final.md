## 1. The one positioning judgment in this doc

Your team's direction is that Lynkk is a conversation memory platform, not a notetaker. In the
Web Store that creates a real tension, because **store search is category-driven** — nobody
browses the store for "conversation memory" (40/mo in Google, no record at all for "conversation
memory platform"; see `06` §1), while `ai meeting notes` and the recorder cluster are what
installers actually type.

**How this listing resolves it:**

| Field | Carries |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The capability words people search — notes, recorder, platform names |
| **Summary** | The objection (no bot) + a first hint of memory |
| **Description** | Memory positioning, from the second paragraph on |

**And one wording rule, same as the website (`06` §4):** the listing says **"AI meeting notes"**,
never **"AI note taker."** The first is a capability Lynkk has; the second is the category your
team is rejecting. Semantically the same cluster to Google and to store search, honest either
way.

- `ai note taker for zoom` — 480/mo, SD 33

First ~45 characters visible in store search cards: `Lynkk: AI Meeting Notes & Recorder for Meet…`
— both capability terms inside the window.

### Alternatives

| Variant | Chars | Trade |
|---|---|---|
| `Lynkk: AI Meeting Notes, Recorder & Memory for Meet, Zoom, Teams` | 64 | Adds "Memory" to the Name. Costs nothing in search and signals the positioning — **pick this if your team wants the category word visible.** |
| `Lynkk: Conversation Memory & AI Meeting Notes — Meet, Zoom, Teams` | 65 | Positioning-led. Honest, but spends the highest-weighted field on a term with ~no store demand. |
| `Lynkk: AI Meeting Notes & Zoom Meeting Recorder — No Bot Joins` | 62 | Exact-phrase `zoom meeting recorder` + the objection. Drops Meet and Teams. |

My recommendation is the 57-char version, or the 64-char "& Memory" variant if the team wants
the word in the Name. I'd avoid the fully positioning-led one here — the store is a discovery
surface, and that variant has nothing to discover you by.

Lynkk records your Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams calls from your own
browser and hands back the summary, the decisions and every action item. No

Then it does the part a notetaker doesn't. Every call joins a connected
memory of your conversations — commitments, decisions and people linked

answer, not a video file.

Built for people who live on calls: account executives, SDRs, account
managers, founders running their own pipeline, and the teams around them.


NOTHING JOINS YOUR MEETING

Most AI meeting tools dial into the call as a visible participant. Your
prospect watches "Notetaker" ask to be admitted, and the first minute of a
discovery call goes on explaining a robot instead of building rapport.

Lynkk records from your own browser tab. No extra participant, nothing to
admit, nothing to apologise for.

Your screen is never captured — audio only.
No tab other than the one you pressed Record on is ever touched.
Call audio and your microphone are recorded on separate channels, so speech
is attributed to the right person — useful when you need to know whether the
objection came from your champion or their CFO.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Install Lynkk and sign in at lynkk.ai. No second login, no setup screens.
2. Join your call — Google Meet, Zoom in the browser, or Microsoft Teams.
3. Press Record. Lynkk detects the call and starts capturing.
4. Hang up. Your summary, action items and searchable call land in Lynkk.

• AI meeting notes — what was discussed, decided and left open
• Action items — each commitment with an owner and a date
• Ask anything — put a question to the call and get an answer from the
  recording, with the source linked

WORKS WHERE YOU WORK

• Google Meet — AI meeting notes and call recording, nothing to admit
• Zoom — a Zoom meeting recorder for calls you join in the browser

FOR YOUR ROLE

Account Executives — run the discovery call instead of typing through it.
Every objection, budget number and next step is captured, so your deal notes
are a record rather than a recollection.

SDRs and BDRs — more conversations a day. Qualification notes and next steps

Account Managers and Customer Success — renewals and QBRs stay on record.
What the customer asked for last quarter is one question away.

Sales Managers — coach from what was actually said. And when an account
changes hands, its history transfers with it.

One recording gives you one set of notes. Lynkk connects them.

Commitments, decisions and the people who made them are linked across every
conversation, so the context survives a rep leaving, an account changing
hands, or a deal going quiet for a month. Install it for today's call; within

Your screen is never captured. Lynkk records audio only.
Nothing joins your meeting as a participant.

Free to start — the free plan is enough to use this extension.

Join the meeting in a browser tab and press Record. You don't need to be the

Yes, both. Press Record in the call tab.

No. Audio only, and only the tab you pressed Record on.

Can I ask questions about past calls?
Yes. Ask across your call history in plain language and get an answer with

## 7. ⚠️ Still blocking: the bot page

This listing's hook is "nothing joins your meeting." `lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot` is the only
page Google has indexed for your domain, ranking #39 for `zoom meeting bot`.

A store reviewer who finds it can read this listing as a deceptive claim, and prospects will
notice too. Resolve before submitting: label both explicitly by product if both are real, or
retire and redirect `/features/meeting-bot` if the bot is legacy. The whole bot cluster is
~100/mo, so there is nothing to lose. Raised in `03` §1b, `05` §4 and `07` §4.

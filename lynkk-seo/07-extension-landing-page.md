## 4. ⚠️ The bot contradiction is now unavoidable

This page's entire hook is **"no bot joins."** `lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot` is currently the
**only page Google has indexed for your domain**, ranking #39 for `zoom meeting bot`.

Two pages on the same domain, one selling a meeting bot and one selling the absence of one, is a
trust problem for prospects and a deceptive-claims risk in the store review. **This has to be
resolved before this page ships.** Options:

- **Both products are real** → label each explicitly by product on both pages, and never let
  either imply it describes all of Lynkk.
- **The bot is legacy** → retire or redirect `/features/meeting-bot` to this page. The whole bot
  cluster is ~100/mo, so there's nothing to lose, and you'd inherit its (small) existing signal.

My recommendation is the second, unless the bot is actively sold. Raised in `03` §1b and `05` §4;
this is the third time it's blocked a deliverable.

## FIELD 3 — Description  (~5,000 / 16,000 characters)

```
RECORD YOUR CALLS. GET THE NOTES. KEEP THE MEMORY.

Lynkk records your Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams calls from your own
browser and hands back the summary, the decisions and every action item. No
bot joins your meeting. No screen capture. One click.

Then it does the part a notetaker doesn't. Every call joins a connected
memory of your conversations — commitments, decisions and people linked
across weeks — so you can ask what someone said in March and get an answer,
not a video file.

Built for people who live on calls: account executives, SDRs, account
managers, founders running their own pipeline, and the teams around them.


NOTHING JOINS YOUR MEETING

Most AI meeting tools dial into the call as a visible participant. Your
prospect watches "Notetaker" ask to be admitted, and the first minute of a
discovery call goes on explaining a robot instead of building rapport.

Lynkk records from your own browser tab. No extra participant, nothing to
admit, nothing to apologise for.

Your screen is never captured — audio only.
No tab other than the one you pressed Record on is ever touched.
Call audio and your microphone are recorded on separate channels, so speech
is attributed to the right person — useful when you need to know whether the
objection came from your champion or their CFO.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Install Lynkk and sign in at lynkk.ai. No second login, no setup screens.
2. Join your call — Google Meet, Zoom in the browser, or Microsoft Teams.
3. Press Record. Lynkk detects the call and starts capturing.
4. Hang up. Your summary, action items and searchable call land in Lynkk.


WHAT YOU GET FROM EVERY CALL

• AI meeting notes — what was discussed, decided and left open
• Action items — each commitment with an owner and a date
• Ask anything — put a question to the call and get an answer from the
  recording, with the source linked
• A connected memory — decisions and commitments linked across every
  conversation, so context carries forward instead of being rebuilt
• Searchable history — find that pricing objection from three weeks ago
• Notes ready to paste into your CRM or your follow-up email


WORKS WHERE YOU WORK

• Google Meet — AI meeting notes and call recording, nothing to admit
• Zoom — a Zoom meeting recorder for calls you join in the browser
• Microsoft Teams — AI meeting notes for client and internal calls


FOR YOUR ROLE

Account Executives — run the discovery call instead of typing through it.
Every objection, budget number and next step is captured, so your deal notes
are a record rather than a recollection.

SDRs and BDRs — more conversations a day. Qualification notes and next steps
write themselves, so you move to the next dial, not to a notes doc.

Account Managers and Customer Success — renewals and QBRs stay on record.
What the customer asked for last quarter is one question away.

Sales Managers — coach from what was actually said, not from what got typed
up afterwards.

Founders selling their own product — every early customer call becomes
product input you can go back and read, not a feeling you half-remember.


MORE THAN ONE CALL AT A TIME

One recording gives you one set of notes. Lynkk connects them.

Commitments, decisions and the people who made them are linked across every
conversation, so the context survives a rep leaving, an account changing
hands, or a deal going quiet for a month. Install it for today's call; within
a month you have a memory of the whole account.


PRIVACY AND CONSENT

Your screen is never captured. Lynkk records audio only.
No tab other than the one you pressed Record on is ever touched.
Nothing joins your meeting as a participant.

Recording laws differ by country and state, and many require everyone's
consent. Tell the people on the call that you are recording. Good practice —
and in a sales conversation it builds more trust than it costs.


PRICING

Free to start — the free plan is enough to use this extension.


GET STARTED

Install Lynkk, sign in at lynkk.ai, and press Record on your next call.


FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is there an AI meeting notes tool that doesn't join the meeting?
That's what this is. Lynkk records from your browser, so no bot appears in
the participant list and nobody has to admit one.

How do I record a meeting on Zoom?
Join the meeting in a browser tab and press Record. You don't need to be the
host or on a paid Zoom plan. Tell the others you're recording.

Does Lynkk work with Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?
Yes, both. Press Record in the call tab.

Will my prospect know I'm recording?
Not from a bot in the participant list — there isn't one. You should still
tell them. Many jurisdictions require it, and it's the right way to run a
call.

Does it record my screen or my other tabs?
No. Audio only, and only the tab you pressed Record on.

Can I ask questions about past calls?
Yes. Ask across your call history in plain language and get an answer with
the source conversation linked.

Do I need a Lynkk account?
Yes. Sign in at lynkk.ai — the extension follows that session, so there's no
separate login here.

Is there a free plan?
Yes. The free plan is enough to use this extension.
```

## Optional additions — only if you can confirm them

These are **deliberately left out** of the paste text above, because an unverified claim in a
store listing risks rejection or a refund complaint. Add them only once they're true and
documented.

| Add | Where | Confirm first |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan specifics ("5 calls/month", "10 hours") | PRICING block and the last FAQ | The actual limit. A number converts better than "free to start" — this is the highest-value addition on the list. |
| Encryption / GDPR | PRICING → new line in PRIVACY AND CONSENT | Only with a DPA and privacy policy behind it, and name your retention period. First thing a security reviewer checks. |
| CRM integration | Change "ready to paste into your CRM" → "syncs with your CRM" | That a real integration exists. Do not upgrade this wording otherwise. |
| Transcript | Add a bullet to WHAT YOU GET | Whether a full transcript is surfaced, or only summaries. |

| Zoom desktop app | FAQ | Browser Zoom is what's stated. If the Mac app covers the desktop client, say so and link. |

**Two things to settle before you submit, not after:**

1. **`lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot`.** This listing's hook is "nothing joins your meeting," and
   that page — currently the only page Google has indexed for your domain — ranks for
   `zoom meeting bot`. A reviewer who finds it can treat this listing as a deceptive claim.
   Retire or redirect it, or label both pages explicitly by product.
2. **Make the free plan read identically** here and on lynkk.ai. A mismatch means installs that
   uninstall, and uninstall rate is a store ranking factor.

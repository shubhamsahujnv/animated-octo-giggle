## 4. The bot question — decide before publishing

This is the one thing I can't write around.

- Your Chrome extension's differentiator is that **nothing joins the call**.
- `lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot` is the **only page Google has indexed** for you, ranking #39
  for `zoom meeting bot`.
- Your product summary describes a **Mac app that records both audio channels** — consistent
  with the bot-free approach.

I've kept "no bot to admit" to a single line in the revenue-teams section and flagged the FAQ,
rather than building the page on it. If both products are real, label them explicitly by
product. If the bot is legacy, retire that page — the whole bot cluster is ~100/mo, so there's
nothing to lose. The objection cluster that *is* worth having
(`ai note taker that doesn't join meetings`, 90/mo at SD 30) belongs on the extension's page,
where the claim is unambiguously true.

## 5. Two structural notes

**This copy is a homepage.** It targets the category head terms, which are SD 41–60 — a 6–12
month play needing real domain authority. The traffic in the near term comes from the dedicated
pages in `04` §3, built in this order: follow-up email (8,100 / SD 7), discovery call
(4,400 / SD 25), discovery call template (SD 14) as a lead magnet linking to both.

**Ship it on `lynkk.ai`, not the `chatgpt.site` subdomain.** Authority earned on a shared
third-party host accrues to that host, and you don't control its robots.txt or sitemap — which
is likely why the audit flagged a missing sitemap you can't fix. Full reasoning in `04` §1.

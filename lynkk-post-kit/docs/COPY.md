# Copy rules for Lynkk posts

## Voice

Plain, calm, specific. Write like a person who uses the product and is a little
tired of meetings, not like an ad. Say what Lynkk does, in the words the product
uses. If a sentence would fit any AI product, cut it.

## Hard rules

- **No em dashes (U+2014)**, anywhere in the file. No en dash as a dash either.
  Use a comma, colon, period, parentheses or "and".
- **No AI cadence.** No "not just X, but Y", no "it's not X, it's Y", no
  rhetorical triplets for rhythm, no dash asides.
- **No filler words**: seamless, supercharge, unlock, elevate, empower,
  effortless, revolutionize, game-changing, next-level, cutting-edge, delve.
- **No exclamation marks.**
- **Only true claims.** Every product fact comes from `TRUTHS.md`. No invented
  numbers, customers, quotes or ratings.
- **No competitor names.** "A typical notetaker", "most notetakers".
- **No prices.** "Start free", "Try it free at lynkk.ai".
- **Product name** is Lynkk (never LYNKK, Lynk, Lynkk.ai in running copy). The
  site is lynkk.ai, lowercase.
- British or American spelling, but one per post. US by default.

## Headlines

One statement, then the turn in the second ink. Short words. Full stops.

- "A meeting has three parts. / Most notetakers cover one."
- "Everyone gets Pro. / The company keeps the record."
- "Record any call. / Nothing joins it."
- "Stop taking notes. Start finishing meetings."

Avoid questions as headlines, puns, and "Meet X" or "Introducing".

## Eyebrows

2 to 5 words, sentence case, no full stop: "The meeting, end to end", "What
decides it for teams", "The short version", "Ask Lynkk · Pro".

## Body lines

One idea per line. Lead with the concrete thing: "Send tasks to Jira from the
note, one issue each." beats "Streamline your workflow with Jira."

Mark Pro features "Pro" (in the eyebrow, or "(Pro)" after the feature).

## CTA

Tag: "Start free". Button: "Try it free at lynkk.ai" or "Start free at
lynkk.ai". Mac posts: "Download at lynkk.ai". One button per slide.

## Captions and alt text

Every post ships with `captions.md` next to it:

- **Instagram caption**: 3 to 6 short lines, the same claims as the slides, end
  with "lynkk.ai, link in bio." and 3 to 6 plain hashtags.
- **LinkedIn caption**: a little longer, a short list is fine, end with the URL.
- **Alt text**: one entry per slide, describing what is on it and quoting the
  headline.

The checker runs on the slides, not the captions. Hold captions to the same
rules by hand.

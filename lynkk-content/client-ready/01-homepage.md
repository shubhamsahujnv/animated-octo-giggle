## 5. What conversation intelligence means

**PLAIN ENGLISH**

## What is conversation intelligence?

Conversation intelligence is software that records your customer conversations, understands what was said, and turns it into something useful — searchable notes, decisions, action items and answers you can ask for in plain language. Instead of a recording nobody rewatches, you get something you can act on.

Most tools stop at one call. Lynkk connects every conversation to the account it belongs to, so your team's memory builds over time instead of resetting after each meeting.

## 17. Trust

**YOUR CONVERSATIONS STAY YOURS**

## What happens to a recording.

- **Lynkk doesn't train AI on your conversations.** Not your recordings, not your transcripts, not your notes.
- **A bot everyone can see.** It only joins calls you send it to, appears in the call named after you, and leaves once it's the last one there.

## 19. FAQ

**QUESTIONS**

## Let's clear things up.

**What is conversation intelligence software?**
Software that records and analyses your customer conversations, then turns them into searchable notes, decisions, action items and answers you can ask for in plain language. Lynkk goes further by connecting every conversation to its account, so context builds over time.

**How is Lynkk different from a meeting notetaker?**
A notetaker helps you revisit a call. Lynkk connects your conversations, so you can ask what changed, track a commitment over time, and get answers with the original context. It also helps with follow-ups, action items and, in the Mac app beta, live help on the call.

**Does it work for in-person meetings too?**
Yes. Alongside Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, the Mac app and the browser record conversations in the room. Your notes can include both online and offline conversations.

**Can Lynkk handle Hindi and English in the same call?**
Yes. Lynkk transcribes 17 languages, plus Hinglish and auto-detect, for conversations that move naturally between Hindi and English.

**Can the customer see my Cue suggestions?**
Only if you share your screen. Cue is a panel on your Mac, so it is not part of the call. It is never hidden from screen shares or recordings, so if you share your screen, it shows. Only text leaves your Mac, never a picture. Cue is in beta on Pro, by invite.

**Will Lynkk send follow-ups without my review?**
No. Ask Lynkk drafts follow-ups using the conversation's context. You review the details, edit the message, and send it yourself.

**Does Lynkk sync with my CRM?**
Not yet. Every brief is formatted to copy straight into Salesforce or HubSpot, and action items go to Jira automatically.

**Do you train AI models on my conversations?**
No. Lynkk does not use your recordings, transcripts or notes to train AI models.

**What's included in the free plan?**
Unlimited notes, up to 2 hours each, and your first 5 hours of AI summaries. No credit card required. Pro adds unlimited summaries and Ask Lynkk across every call.

**Where can I learn about data privacy?**
Read the security page and the privacy policy for how your data is collected, used, stored and deleted.

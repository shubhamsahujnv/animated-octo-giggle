## What it means

## What is real-time sales coaching?

Real-time sales coaching gives a rep help during the live call rather than afterwards — surfacing the account's history, the relevant number, and suggested wording on their screen as the conversation happens. The rep gets the answer while the question is still open.

Traditional call coaching software reviews recordings after the fact. By then the deal has already moved.

---

## The problem

## Why coaching after the call is too late

Call reviews are useful. They're also a week late.

By the time a manager has listened back, the deal has moved, the question was fumbled, and the rep has repeated it in four more calls. Nobody can review every call, so most calls are never coached at all.

Reps don't need more feedback. They need help at the moment it's hard.

## How it works

## Four things to know

**01 · Help with this moment**
Leave the box empty and press ⌘↩. Cue reads what's on your screen and what's being said, and tells you what it would do next.

**02 · It listens to the call**
It hears your microphone as *Me* and the call as *Them*, so it knows who said what.

**03 · Modes, with your own notes**
Sales, Support, Interview prep — or your own mode, with notes you paste in. The same question gets a different answer depending on the job.

**04 · Visible, and private**
Never hidden from a screen share. Only text leaves your Mac, never a picture, and nothing is saved unless you choose.

**Shortcuts**
`⌘\` show or hide · `⌘⇧\` listen · `⌘↩` help with your screen · `⌘R` new chat · `⌘←→` move it

## Our position

## We built it to be visible.

There's a category of tool right now that sells itself on being undetectable in a meeting. Cue isn't one of them, on purpose.

Cue is a panel on your Mac, not part of the call. It never hides from a screen share or a recording — so if you share your screen, your customer sees it. We think that's the right trade. A tool you'd have to hide is a tool you shouldn't be using in front of a customer.

What stays private is the content: only text leaves your Mac, never an image of your screen, and nothing is stored unless you ask for it.

---

## For reps

## It helps reps. It doesn't replace them.

Cue doesn't sell for you. It doesn't score you for your manager behind your back. It doesn't replace judgement built over years of hard conversations.

It does the part that was never a test of skill — remembering the right fact at the right second. New reps get to sound like they've been on the account a year. Experienced reps stop losing deals to a number they couldn't recall.

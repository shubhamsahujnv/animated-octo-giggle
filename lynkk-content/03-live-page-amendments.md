# Amendments to the live pages

The three feature pages and `/pricing` are already well written and well optimised. I'm not rewriting them. These are specific, surgical changes only.

---

## `/features/meeting-notes`

**Current H1:** "The AI note taker that writes your meeting notes when the call ends."
**Current title:** AI Note Taker for Meeting Notes & Minutes | Lynkk

This page is strong. The H1 is keyword-bearing and specific, the format — the page *is* one Lynkk note, with margin annotations explaining what Lynkk did — is genuinely original. Leave the structure alone.

**Three additions:**

**1. A "what is an AI note taker?" block.** The page explains what Lynkk's note taker does but never defines the category. `ai note taker` is 27,100/mo in the US, and while that head term isn't winnable at this authority, the definition block is what AI Overviews pull for the dozens of long-tail variants. Suggested copy:

> ### What is an AI note taker?
>
> An AI note taker joins or records your meeting, transcribes what was said, and writes the notes for you — a summary, the decisions, the action items and a searchable transcript. You stay in the conversation instead of typing through it.

**2. Make the free tier's shape explicit on this page.** Someone landing here from search doesn't know what they get. One line near the first CTA: *"Free records and transcribes every meeting you have, and summarises your first 5 hours."*

**3. Add the in-person angle to the page, not just the homepage.** `ai note taker for in person meetings` is **480/mo with 100% paid competition** — advertisers are fighting over it, which means it converts, and most bot-based competitors can't serve it. This page is the right home for it, as an H2: *"An AI note taker for in-person meetings, too."*

## `/features/ask-your-meetings`

**Current H1:** "Six calls, five weeks, one answer."
**Current title:** Ask Your Meetings: One Answer Across Every Call | Lynkk

The Acme renewal walkthrough — six calls over five weeks, each claim footnoted to the minute it was said — is the best single piece of product storytelling on the site. Don't touch it.

**Two changes:**

**1. Fix the naming.** This product is called three things: "Ask Lynkk" (in-app and homepage), "Ask your meetings" (URL and page title), "Conversational brain" (footer). Pick one. My recommendation is **Ask Lynkk** — it's the in-app name, it's brandable, and it's what users will search for once they've used it. Keep the URL as-is for the keyword, but make every visible label "Ask Lynkk".

**2. Add a definition block.** Same reason as above:

> ### What does it mean to ask your meetings?
>
> Instead of searching for a recording and watching it back, you ask a question in plain language — about a customer, a person or a project — and get one answer drawn from every call where it came up, with the date and minute of each part.

## `/features/workspaces`

**Current H1:** "Everyone gets Pro. The company keeps the record."
**Current title:** Workspaces: Team Meeting Notes with Pro for Everyone | Lynkk

Good page. The honesty about admin visibility — every note states whether admins can see it, and anything can be marked Personal — is handled well, and the audit-log detail (opening a member's note is recorded) is exactly the kind of thing that wins a procurement conversation.

**Two additions:**

**1. Say the seat limit in the meta description and above the fold.** "Up to 10" is a real constraint and a real disqualifier. It's currently in the sub-line; it should be impossible to miss, or you'll waste demo time on 40-person teams.

**2. Add the shared-memory argument.** The page sells the admin and billing case well but under-sells the product case: a workspace means a question can be answered from a colleague's call, not just your own. The homepage already demonstrates this ("What's blocking our open deals?" answered from two different reps' notes). That demo belongs on this page too — it's the reason a team upgrades, rather than each person buying Pro separately.

## `/pricing`

**Current H1:** "Lynkk pricing: free to start, $15 a month for Pro."
**Current title:** Pricing: Free AI Meeting Notes, Pro at $15 a Month | Lynkk

Price in the title and H1 is the right call — it pre-qualifies and it earns the click from people comparing.

**One change, and it matters:**

The 5 free hours of AI summaries is a **lifetime** allowance, not monthly. The page does explain this — *"over the life of your account, not per month. Deleting notes doesn't give hours back"* — but it's below the fold, under a heading that reads "After the 5 hours".

Move it up into the Free plan card itself. Change:

> Your first 5 hours of AI summaries

to:

> Your first 5 hours of AI summaries, for the life of the account

A user who assumes it resets monthly and discovers otherwise in week three feels misled, even though the page said so. One clause prevents it.

## Site-wide

**1. One keyword gap worth closing.** The twelve `/compare/` pages cover "lynkk vs X" intent. They don't cover the bigger half: **`gong competitors` — 320/mo, difficulty 14, $80.49 CPC.** That search results page is held entirely by numbered roundups ("11 Best Gong Alternatives", "Top 6 Gong competitors"), and sites at domain authority 10, 23 and 27 rank there. A 1-v-1 page won't win it. One roundup post that includes Lynkk honestly alongside the others is the best-value page left to write. Same pattern applies to `otter ai alternative` (480/mo, difficulty 34).

**2. Resolve the language count.** Homepage meta says 17 languages; body, FAQ and pricing say 15+. One of them is out of date.

**3. The homepage H2s still carry no searchable terms.** Thirteen of them, all brand poetry. The eyebrow-label fix in `01-homepage.md` needs no design change.

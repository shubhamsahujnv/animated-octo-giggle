## 5. What conversation intelligence means

**PLAIN ENGLISH**

## What is conversation intelligence?

Conversation intelligence is software that records your customer conversations, understands what was said, and turns it into something useful — searchable notes, tracked commitments, CRM updates and coaching. Instead of a recording nobody rewatches, you get answers you can act on.

Most conversation intelligence tools stop at analysing one call. Lynkk connects every conversation to the account it belongs to, so your team's memory builds over time instead of resetting after each meeting.

## 16. FAQ

**GOOD QUESTIONS.**

## Let's clear things up.

**What is conversation intelligence software?**
Conversation intelligence software records and analyses your customer conversations, then turns them into searchable notes, tracked action items, CRM updates and coaching. Lynkk goes further by connecting every conversation to its account, so context builds over time.

**How is Lynkk different from a meeting notetaker?**
A notetaker helps you revisit a call. Lynkk connects conversations across an account, so you can ask what changed, track a commitment over time, and get answers with the original context. It also handles follow-ups, CRM updates and live coaching.

**Does it work for in-person meetings too?**
Yes. Alongside Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, Lynkk captures in-person and phone conversations through mobile recording. Your account memory can include both.

**Can I use an AI meeting assistant without a bot joining?**
With Lynkk you choose per meeting — bot or no bot, keep the recording or keep only the notes. [TO CONFIRM]

**Can the customer see my CUE suggestions?**
No. CUE is private and visible only to you during the call.

**Will Lynkk send follow-ups without my review?**
No. Lynkk drafts follow-ups from the conversation's context. You review the details, edit the message, and send when you're ready.

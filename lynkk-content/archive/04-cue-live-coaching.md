## Definition

## What is real-time sales coaching?

Real-time sales coaching gives a rep help during the live call rather than afterwards — surfacing account context, suggested responses to objections, and competitor positioning on their screen as the conversation happens. The rep gets the answer while the question is still in the air.

Traditional call coaching software reviews recordings after the fact. By then the deal has already moved.

---

## The problem

## Why coaching after the call is too late

Call reviews are useful. They're also a week late.

By the time a manager has listened back, the deal has moved, the objection was fumbled, and the rep has repeated it in four more calls. Nobody can review every call, so most calls are never coached at all.

Reps don't need more feedback. They need help at the moment it's hard.

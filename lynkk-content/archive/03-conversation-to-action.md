## Definition

## What is an agentic AI sales assistant?

An agentic AI sales assistant does more than summarise — it completes the work a conversation creates. It drafts the follow-up, assigns the action items, updates your CRM fields and raises the ticket, then waits for your approval before anything is sent or saved.

The difference from an ordinary AI assistant is simple: one tells you, the other does it.

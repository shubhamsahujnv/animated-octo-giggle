## Definition

## What is an AI meeting assistant?

An AI meeting assistant helps with the whole meeting rather than just the notes — briefing you beforehand, answering questions afterwards, and preparing the follow-up work. A note-taker produces a summary. An assistant produces a prepared you, answered questions, and work that's already started.

---

## The problem

## Note-taking was never the hard part

There are dozens of AI note-takers. Most do one thing: you have a meeting, you get a summary.

But the summary was never hard.

Walking into a meeting already knowing where you left off is hard. Getting a number you can't recall while the customer waits is hard. Finding out what happened in the meeting you missed without watching fifty minutes of video is hard. Knowing what you promised three people last week is hard.

That's an assistant. The rest is transcription.

## Sources

## Every answer shows its working.

Ask about a customer, a person or a project and Lynkk reads every call where it came up — including the ones whose title never mentioned it — then puts the story together with the date and the minute of each part.

Hover a citation to find its call. Click it and the recording plays from that second.

Answers come only from your own notes. Nothing is invented, and nothing comes from outside your account.

## Summary Shapes

## The same call, shaped for the job. `Pro`

A sales call and a user interview shouldn't produce the same notes. Pick a shape and the summary reorganises around what matters.

**Brief** — Key takeaways · Topics · Action items · Decisions
**Sales Call** — Customer · Stakeholders · Pain points · Budget
**General Meeting** — Overview · Discussion · Decisions · Action items
**Customer Interview** — Goals · Pain points · Current workflow · Feature requests

Switching never changes the transcript or the tasks.

## Shared knowledge

## Your team's memory, not just yours

This is the part a personal note-taker can't do.

In a workspace, a question can be answered from a colleague's call as well as your own — *"what's blocking our open deals?"* answered from two different reps' notes, each with its source.

## FAQ

**What is an AI meeting assistant?**
A tool that helps with the whole meeting rather than just the notes — preparing you beforehand, answering questions after, and getting the follow-up work started.

**Which AI meeting assistant is best?**
Depends what you need. For tidy notes from one call, the simple note-takers are fine and cheap. If you need meetings to connect to each other and to an account, you need memory. That's what Lynkk is built for.

**Is Ask Lynkk included on the free plan?**
No. Free records, transcribes and searches every meeting, and summarises your first 5 hours. Ask Lynkk is on Pro, at $19 a month.

**Can it answer questions about meetings I wasn't in?**
In a workspace, yes — within the access rules on each note.

**Where do the answers come from?**
Only your own notes. Every answer carries the call, date and minute behind each claim.

**Does it work for in-person meetings too?**
Yes. The Mac app and the browser record conversations in the room, and those notes are searchable and askable like any other.

## Definition

## What is an AI meeting assistant?

An AI meeting assistant helps with the whole meeting rather than just the notes — briefing you beforehand, answering questions during the call, and finishing the follow-up work afterwards. A note-taker produces a summary; an assistant produces prepared you, answered questions and completed admin.

---

## The problem

## Note-taking was never the hard part

There are dozens of AI note-takers. Most do one thing: you have a meeting, you get a summary.

But the summary was never hard.

Walking into a meeting already knowing where you left off is hard. Getting a number you can't recall while the customer waits is hard. Finding out what happened in the meeting you missed without watching fifty minutes of video is hard. Clearing the admin so it doesn't pile up to Friday is hard.

That's an assistant. The rest is transcription.

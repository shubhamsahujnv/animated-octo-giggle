## Definition

## What is an agentic AI sales assistant?

An agentic AI sales assistant does more than summarise — it prepares the work a conversation creates. It writes the follow-up, assigns the action items, and opens the ticket, then stops and waits for you.

The difference from an ordinary AI assistant is simple: one tells you, the other does it. The difference from an agent you'd be nervous about is simpler still: nothing leaves your hands until you say so.

## Tasks

## Every action item, on one board.

Action items are usually a bullet list nobody owns. Lynkk gives each one the person who agreed to it, the date they agreed to, and the moment in the call where it happened.

They land on a board you can work from: list or kanban, with open, in progress, in review and done. Overdue count, completion rate, filter by assignee.

Send any of them to Jira and the issue stays linked to the call.

`Tasks board` `Owners and dates` `Jira` — **on the free plan**

## Email summary

## Send the recap without writing it.

Two different jobs, two different tools.

**The email summary** goes to the people who were on the call. Lynkk picks up the participants, you choose the right address for each, and nothing sends until you confirm.

**The drafted follow-up** is for when you need to say something, not just recap. Ask Lynkk for it and you get a draft built from the numbers and the promises in the call. You copy it into your email and send it.

## FAQ

**Will Lynkk send follow-ups without my review?**
No. Ask Lynkk drafts the follow-up using the conversation's context. You review the details, edit the message, and send it yourself.

**Does Lynkk sync with my CRM?**
Not yet. Every brief is formatted to copy straight into Salesforce or HubSpot, and action items go to Jira automatically.

**How accurate are the action items?**
They come from what was said, and each one carries the moment in the call where it was agreed, so you can check it in a click.

**Is the Tasks board on the free plan?**
Yes. The Tasks board, decisions and sending action items to Jira are all included on Free.

**Does it work for in-person meetings?**
Yes. The Mac app and the browser record conversations in the room, and those notes behave exactly like a video call's.

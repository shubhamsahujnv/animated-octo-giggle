## 0. What the SERP data changed — read before you brief a writer

I ran SERP analysis on the five target keywords. One finding overrides a lot of standard advice:

### Finding 1: Your product pages cannot win the head terms. The SERPs don't want product pages.

| Keyword | What actually ranks | Page-1 DA range |
|---|---|---|
| `ai sales assistant` (590/mo) | **Listicles.** "The 12 best AI sales assistant tools", "15 Best AI Sales Assistants", "What is AI sales assistant software?" | **16–49** |
| `ai sales coaching` (390/mo) | Mix — Dialpad's product page (DA 65), plus "15 best sales coaching AI tools", "13 best", "6 Best" | **5–92** |
| `account intelligence` (90/mo) | **Definition guides.** "What is account intelligence? A complete guide" — plus 2 product pages (Pylon DA 34, SalesPort DA 10) | **10–92** |
| `conversation intelligence platform` (880/mo) | Gong, Salesloft, Dialpad product pages + IBM/ZoomInfo definitions | **30–93** |
| `gong competitors` (320/mo) | **Vendor listicles.** "11 Best Gong Alternatives", "Top 6 Gong competitors", "10 Best Gong Alternatives & Competitors in 2026" | **10–91** |

**The good news is bigger than the bad.** Look at those DA ranges. On `ai sales assistant`: trellus.ai ranks #4 at **DA 25**, jeeva.ai #9 at **DA 26**, runo.ai #10 at **DA 24**, meera.ai #11 at **DA 16**. On `gong competitors`: oliv.ai #6 at **DA 27** pulling 18 clicks/mo, askelephant.ai #9 at **DA 23**, industry-lens #13 at **DA 10**. On `ai sales coaching`, sales-coach.ai ranks #13 at **DA 5**.

Low authority is not the barrier. **Wrong page format is.** A product page competing against twelve listicles loses regardless of how good the copy is.

**So the structure is two-layer:**

1. **Product pages** (below) — optimised for mid-tail, buyer-intent, lower-competition terms they can actually win, and built to convert the traffic.
2. **Supporting listicles/guides** — these capture the head terms and link into the product pages. This is exactly how oliv.ai at DA 27 and askelephant at DA 23 get traffic. §6 lists them.

`conversation intelligence platform` is the one I'd drop as a homepage target — DA 30 (Jiminny) is the floor, and you're at 1. The homepage targets your brand plus `conversation intelligence software for sales teams` instead, and the head term gets attacked later with a guide.

### Finding 2: AI Overview and People Also Ask appear on 4 of 5 SERPs

`conversation intelligence platform`, `account intelligence`, `ai sales coaching` and `gong competitors` all show an AI Overview at position 1, and PAA blocks. That means **a direct-answer paragraph immediately under each H2 is worth more than any keyword density tactic.** Every "what is" block below is written as a 40–55 word definition in the first two sentences, which is the shape that gets pulled.

### Finding 3: `account intelligence` is half-navigational

Salesforce's help doc ranks #8 and Oracle's Eloqua doc ranks #11. A chunk of that 90/mo wants a CRM setting, not a product. So Account Memory targets `account intelligence software` and `b2b account intelligence` as primaries instead of the bare term — same audience, none of the noise.

## What drives cost

- **Scope and stage** — a first MVP versus a platform replacing a system in production
- **Integration surface** — how many existing systems must be read from and written to
- **Compliance load** — the controls and evidence your sector requires
- **Starting state** — a clean build, or a legacy estate to work around safely
- **Model** — fixed-scope project with milestone commercials, versus an embedded squad
- **Handover depth** — how much documentation and enablement your own team needs

## FAQ

**What does Pixorama do?**
Pixorama is an engineering-led software company. We design, build and operate cloud platforms, AI and
data products, mobile apps and security-hardened systems for enterprises and funded startups. In
short: we turn an idea or a stalled roadmap into shipped, scalable software you own.

**How do engagements and pricing work?**
Choose the model that fits your stage: outcome-based fixed-scope projects, a dedicated squad embedded
in your roadmap, specialist augmentation for a critical capability, or advisory and architecture
reviews. Every model is staffed with senior people and run against clear, measurable outcomes.

**What industries do you work with?**
Regulated, high-stakes sectors: financial services, healthcare and life sciences, retail and
eCommerce, logistics and mobility, SaaS and technology, public sector, energy and utilities, and
education. Our discovery process gets us productive in a new domain quickly.

**Can you modernise a legacy system without stopping delivery?**
That is the default approach — incremental replacement with a working system at every step, rather
than a big-bang rewrite. See
[Legacy application modernization](/blog/legacy-application-modernization).

**Where is Pixorama based, and do you work remotely?**
We are remote-first and senior-led, with distributed teams that align to your time zone and work
with clients across regions. Founded in 2022, we operate as a hands-on engineering partner rather
than a body shop.

## Our Five-Step AI Development Process

1. **Pick the question set first** The decisions and answers the system must get right, written down before any model is chosen.
2. **Get the data ready** Pipelines, permissions and quality work, because retrieval is only as good as what it reads.
3. **Build the evaluation set before the feature** A scored set of real questions, so quality is measured rather than asserted.
4. **Ship a narrow slice to production** One evaluable workflow inside the first fortnight, with guardrails and an audit trail.
5. **Monitor quality, cost and drift** Live monitoring and regression evaluation on every change after launch.

## What AI Development Services Cost

- **Data readiness**: clean, accessible, permissioned data, or work needed before any AI is useful
- **Scope**, one grounded assistant versus a multi-workflow agent system
- **Accuracy bar**, the evaluation and guardrail work scales with the consequence of being wrong
- **Integration surface**, how many systems the AI must read from and write to
- **Compliance load**, audit, retention and explainability requirements in your sector
- **Run cost**, inference and retrieval cost, which we design against rather than discover later

## Frequently Asked Questions About AI Development Services

**Can you build AI into our product?**
Yes. We build production-grade AI on top of your data: LLM assistants and copilots,
retrieval-augmented generation, agents and machine-learning models, all with guardrails, evaluation
and monitoring. We start from the outcome and the data, not the demo, so what ships is safe and
measurable.

**RAG or fine-tuning: which do we need?**
Usually RAG first. It grounds answers in your current content, cites sources, and updates when your
data updates. Fine-tuning earns its place for form and behaviour, rarely for knowledge.
See [RAG vs fine-tuning](/blog/rag-vs-fine-tuning).

**How do you stop it hallucinating?**
Permission-aware retrieval, grounding with citations, explicit refusal behaviour, and an evaluation
set that runs on every change. The goal is a system whose failure modes are known and monitored.

**Do we own the AI system you build?**
Completely: code, infrastructure, prompts, evaluation sets and documentation, with handover
designed in from the start.

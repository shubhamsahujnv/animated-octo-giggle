## FAQ

**Do you do SEO as well as build the site?**
Yes. Technical SEO, content structure and analytics are part of the same engagement. A site that
launches and cannot be found has not finished.

**What is AI answer readiness, and do we need it?**
Structuring pages so assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can quote and cite them: an
answer-ready opening, scannable facts, schema and a direct FAQ. It matters because an increasing
share of buyers ask an assistant before they search, and those visits often arrive as direct traffic.

**Can you migrate our existing site without losing traffic?**
Yes. Migration work is redirect mapping, canonical and indexation handling, and monitoring after
launch. It is the part that is usually skipped and most often causes a traffic drop.

**Will we be able to edit the site ourselves?**
Yes. You get full ownership of the code and content, documented, on a stack your own team can edit.
There is no lock-in by obscurity.

**How long before we see results?**
Build work ships in the first fortnight and continues in short cycles. Search results compound over
months. Anyone promising rankings on a fixed date is guessing.

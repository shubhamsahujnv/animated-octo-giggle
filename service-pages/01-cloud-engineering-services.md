## Who it's for

- Enterprises and funded startups in financial services, healthcare and life sciences, logistics and

## FAQ

Moved from the homepage's combined FAQ so each question lives on exactly one page.

**How do you keep software secure and compliant?**
Security is engineered in, not bolted on. We use zero-trust architecture, automate evidence
collection, and harden the delivery pipeline so SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness becomes continuous
rather than an end-of-cycle scramble.

**How quickly can you start delivering?**
Engagements are senior-led from day one with no ramp-up tax. We ship a production increment inside
the first fortnight, then keep shipping in short cycles.

**Do we own the code and the infrastructure?**
Completely. You get full ownership of the code, infrastructure and documentation, with handover
designed in from the start. There is no lock-in by obscurity.

**Which cloud providers do you work with?**
`[CONFIRM: AWS / Azure / GCP, and any partner status. Do not claim a partnership that does not
exist, but if one does exist, it is a trust signal currently missing from the site.]`

**Can you take over a platform someone else built?**
Yes. Advisory and architecture reviews exist for exactly this. We assess what is there, agree a
target architecture, and work to it incrementally rather than demanding a rewrite.

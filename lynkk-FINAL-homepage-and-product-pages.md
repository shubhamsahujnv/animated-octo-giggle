## FAQ

**What is an AI meeting assistant?**
A tool that helps with the whole meeting rather than just the notes — preparing you beforehand, answering questions during, and finishing the follow-up work after.

**Which AI meeting assistant is best?**
Depends what you need. For tidy notes from one call, the simple note-takers are fine and cheap. If you need meetings to connect to each other, to accounts and to your CRM, you need memory. That's what Lynkk is built for.

**Does it work for in-person meetings too?**
Yes. Alongside Zoom, Google Meet and Teams, Lynkk captures in-person and phone conversations through mobile recording.

**Can I use it without a bot joining?**

**Can it answer questions about meetings I wasn't in?**

**Can I ask it things by voice during a call?**
Yes — ask out loud and get an answer by voice.

## Implementation notes

1. **`og:url` on the homepage currently points at the staging domain** — change it, or social shares will expose the staging URL. Also missing: `og:image`, canonical tag.
2. **Eyebrow labels are doing the SEO work.** Your thirteen H2s are great lines with no searchable terms in them. Rather than rewrite the headlines, the keyword sits in the eyebrow slot you already have above each section. No design change needed.
3. **One primary keyword per page**, so the pages don't compete:
   - Home → `conversation intelligence platform`
   - Account Memory → `account intelligence`
   - Conversation to Action → `ai sales assistant`
   - CUE → `ai sales coaching`
   - Personal Assistant → `ai meeting assistant`
4. **Add FAQPage schema** to all five FAQ blocks.
5. **Internal links** are marked at the foot of each product page. Five connected pages beat five islands.
6. **Name your CRMs.** "CRM integration" with no names makes buyers assume theirs isn't supported.
7. **Every `[PLACEHOLDER]` needs a real name, number or quote — or the line gets cut.** Don't publish invented customer results. You're already careful to label mock data "illustrative"; hold that same line in the stories.

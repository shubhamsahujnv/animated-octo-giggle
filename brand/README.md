# The Green Solve — Brand Kit

Based on the website’s existing identity, checked 9 October 2026.

## Identity
The Green Solve (TGS) is a B2B carbon credit marketplace facilitator connecting buyers to third-party-issued and certified credits. TGS is not a registry or verifier and does not issue, validate or verify credits itself.

## Colours
| Colour | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Teal | #02403D | Primary brand, headings, navigation |
| Lime | #E2FF4D | Accent and primary calls to action |
| Taupe | #D8CAC3 | Secondary surfaces |
| Mauve | #665458 | Supporting text |
| Cream | #FFFDF8 | Main background |

These are the website’s named brand colours. Some interface shades are derived from HSL tokens and may differ slightly from these base values.

## Typography
- Headings: Libre Baskerville, regular and bold; italic for selective emphasis.
- Body: Helvetica Neue / Helvetica, with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks.
- Fonts are not bundled. Obtain Libre Baskerville from Google Fonts; use Helvetica only with a valid licence or an available system font.

## Logos
- logo-dark.png: original transparent Deep Teal logo for light backgrounds.
- logo-light.png: original transparent Lime logo for dark backgrounds.
- Both originals are 1920 × 1080 PNG files with built-in transparent margins.
- Keep proportions intact. Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, add effects or place on busy imagery. Avoid enlarging beyond usable raster resolution.
- No vector master or formally approved clear-space/minimum-size standard was available in the project.

## Voice and positioning
Professional, clear, conservative, evidence-led and appropriate for sustainability managers, auditors and consultants. Avoid consumer gimmicks and promotional hype.

Required disclosures:
“Carbon credits do not eliminate emissions and should be used as part of a broader decarbonization strategy.”
“Credits listed on this platform are issued and certified by third-party standards. The Green Solve acts as a marketplace facilitator.”

Never use “TGS-verified” or imply TGS certifies projects. Avoid unsupported environmental guarantees.

## Included
Brand guide PDF, original transparent PNG logos, this written guide and machine-readable colour values. No new logo or identity has been created.

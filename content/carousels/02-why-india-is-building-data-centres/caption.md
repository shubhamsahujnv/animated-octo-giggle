## LinkedIn caption

Why is India building so many data centres? Five forces stand out:

1. Users: 958 million active internet users, 57% of them rural (IAMAI–Kantar, 2025)
2. Digital life: 24.07 billion UPI transactions in September 2026 alone (NPCI)
3. Data rules: RBI's 2018 payment-data storage rule and the DPDP Act 2023, with DPDP Rules phasing in through 2027
4. AI: the ₹10,372 crore IndiaAI Mission, and AI as the IEA's biggest driver of data centre electricity growth
5. Connectivity: subsea cables landing on the coast. Mumbai and Chennai host nearly three-quarters of capacity (CBRE)

Facilities going up now will operate for decades. Locking in clean power, efficient cooling and circular hardware at the design stage is far cheaper than retrofitting later.

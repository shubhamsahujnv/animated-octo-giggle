Reason 01 of 05

More people
online.

958M
active internet
users in 2025
57% rural

Most new users now come from rural India, and each one adds to the data the country stores and serves.

Room to grow: about 579 million Indians are still not active online, so demand will keep rising.
Source: IAMAI–Kantar, Internet in India Report 2025.

04 / 09
Reason 03 of 05

Rules to keep
data close to home.

2018
RBI requires payment-system data to be stored only in India.
2023
The Digital Personal Data Protection Act sets national rules for handling and transferring personal data.
2025 → 2027
The DPDP Rules phase in, and companies plan where their data will live.
The result: more data stored and processed inside India, which means more local data centres.
05 / 09
Reason 04 of 05

The AI race.

AI needs specialised chips that run hot and draw far more power than everyday servers.

₹10,372 cr
IndiaAI Mission (2024), including shared AI computing capacity

Globally, the IEA names AI as the biggest driver of data centre electricity growth to 2030.

More AI means more computing, and more electricity. Clean power matters more than ever.
Sources: Government of India, IndiaAI Mission (approved 2024); IEA, Energy and AI (2025).
06 / 09
Reason 05 of 05

Cables that land
on our coasts.

Undersea internet cables land in coastal cities. That's a big reason Mumbai and Chennai host nearly three-quarters of India's data centre capacity.

Source: CBRE (Mumbai 53%, Chennai 20% of operational capacity, Sept 2025).

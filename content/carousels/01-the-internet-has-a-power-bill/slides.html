First, the basics

Wait, what is a
data centre?

A building packed with computers called servers. They store, process and deliver the data behind banking, streaming, cloud apps and AI, every second of every day.

Store
Process
Deliver
The "cloud" is not in the sky. It's thousands of these buildings, switched on 24×7.
02 / 09
Why the power bill is so big

Two things never
switch off.

01
The servers
Running day and night, they turn almost all the electricity they use into heat.
02
The cooling
Fans, chillers and pumps work non-stop to stop that heat from damaging the servers.

More data means more servers, more heat and more power.

03 / 09
The global picture

Demand is set to
more than double.

In 2024, data centres used about 1.5% of the world's electricity. That's on track for more than 2× by 2030.

Source: IEA, Energy and AI (2025): global data centre electricity consumption, base case.

06 / 09
The hopeful part

India's grid is
getting cleaner, fast.

More than half of India's installed power capacity is now non-fossil.

300 GW+ installed, over 60% of the 500 GW goal for 2030.

Honest note: installed capacity isn't the same as generation, and coal still supplies most of India's electricity. But every new clean gigawatt makes the cloud cleaner too.
Source: MNRE, non-fossil installed capacity as of 31 July 2026 (300.5 GW).

Three levers for a

Efficient cooling

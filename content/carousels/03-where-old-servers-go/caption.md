## Instagram caption

The world threw away 62 million tonnes of electronics in a single year. Only about 1 in 5 tonnes was properly recycled. ♻️

Data centres add to the pile: servers, drives, switches and batteries are replaced every few years. Inside them is both treasure (gold, copper) and trouble (lead, mercury).

The hopeful part: India's E-Waste Rules make producers responsible for recycling through registered recyclers. Extend, reuse, recover, recycle.

## LinkedIn caption

62 million tonnes of e-waste were generated globally in 2022, and only 22.3% was documented as properly recycled (UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024). India's reported e-waste nearly doubled between 2017–18 and 2024–25 (CPCB data).

Data centre hardware is part of this stream. India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022, in force since April 2023, set Extended Producer Responsibility targets met only through CPCB-registered recyclers.

Four questions sustainability teams can ask IT partners:
• Do you offer end-of-life take-back?
• Are your recyclers registered on the CPCB portal?
• How is data securely wiped?
• Can refurbished equipment meet some of our needs?

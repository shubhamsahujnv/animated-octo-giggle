The global picture

Only about 1 in 5 tonnes
is properly recycled.

In 2022, just 22.3% of e-waste was documented as collected and recycled properly.

E-waste is on track to reach 82 million tonnes a year by 2030.

E-waste is growing almost five times faster than documented recycling.
Source: UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024 (ITU/UNITAR).

What's inside a server

Treasure and
trouble, in one box.

Worth recovering

  • Gold
  • Copper
  • Aluminium
  • Rare earths

Harmful if dumped

  • Lead
  • Mercury
  • Cadmium
  • Flame retardants
Recycled properly, old hardware becomes a resource. Dumped, it becomes a hazard.

The hopeful part

India's rules now make
producers responsible.

The E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022, in force since April 2023, are built on Extended Producer Responsibility.

01
Producers get yearly recycling targets
02
Targets are met only through registered recyclers
03
Everything is tracked on the CPCB online portal
Source: MoEFCC, E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 (in force 1 April 2023).

The catch

Data centres never sleep.
The sun does.

Solar alone can't power a 24×7 load. The answer is a mix: solar, plus wind, which often blows into the evening, plus battery storage.

06 / 09
The honest note

The gold standard:
clean power every hour.

Buying enough renewable energy over a year isn't the same as running on clean power around the clock.

GOOD
Annual matching
Total clean energy bought equals total use over the year
BEST
24×7 matching
Clean energy covers demand in every single hour
Why it matters: coal still generates most of India's electricity, especially at night. Hourly matching drives investment in wind and storage.

## LinkedIn caption

Can India's cloud run on India's sunshine?

India's non-fossil power capacity reached 300.5 GW by 31 July 2026, more than 54% of total installed capacity, with solar alone at 164.6 GW (MNRE). FY 2025–26 saw a record 55.3 GW of clean capacity added.

For data centres, the policy door is open too. The Green Energy Open Access Rules, 2022:
→ cut the eligibility threshold from 1 MW to 100 kW
→ set a 15-day approval window
→ allow surplus green energy to be banked

The real challenge is round-the-clock supply. Annual renewable matching is a good start; 24×7 hourly matching is the gold standard, and it drives investment in wind and storage.

Three questions worth asking your cloud or colocation provider:
1. What share of your power in India is renewable?
2. Is it matched annually or hour by hour?
3. Through which route: green power contracts, own plants or utility green tariffs?

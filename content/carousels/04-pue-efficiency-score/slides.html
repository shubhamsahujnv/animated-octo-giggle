Meet PUE

Power Usage
Effectiveness.

It compares the energy a whole data centre uses with the energy that actually reaches the computers.

PUE =
Total facility energy
computers + cooling + lighting + power losses
IT equipment energy
servers, storage and network only

A perfect score is 1.0: every unit of power goes to computing.

03 / 09
The global picture

The world average is
stuck above 1.5.

Average PUE has barely moved in six years, held back by older buildings and hot climates. The most efficient large facilities report figures close to 1.1.

Source: Uptime Institute, Global Data Center Survey 2025 (weighted average PUE above 1.5). Chart is illustrative of the flat trend.

The India challenge

Heat and humidity
make it harder here.

Hot summers
Cooling systems work harder for more of the year
Humid coasts
Mumbai and Chennai, India's biggest hubs, are coastal and humid
So design matters more
Smart engineering can close the gap with cooler countries
05 / 09
The hopeful part

Four proven ways to
bring PUE down.

Contain the airflow
Separate hot and cold aisles so cool air isn't wasted
Run a little warmer
Raise temperature setpoints within safe equipment limits
Cool with liquid
Direct liquid cooling handles dense AI racks efficiently
Cut power losses
Efficient UPS and power systems waste less energy

The honest caveat

PUE isn't the
whole story.

A data centre can be very efficient and still run on coal power, or use a lot of water. Look at three numbers together.

WUE

RE%
Renewable share
How much of the electricity is clean

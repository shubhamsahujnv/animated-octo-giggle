## LinkedIn caption

The cloud has an address, and increasingly it's in India.

India's data centre capacity reached roughly 1.5 GW in 2025 (MeitY), with Mumbai hosting about half. Industry projections put capacity in the top seven cities above 4.5 GW by 2030 (Colliers).

Globally, data centres used ~1.5% of the world's electricity in 2024, and the IEA expects that to more than double by 2030.

The opportunity: India's non-fossil capacity has crossed 300 GW, more than 54% of installed capacity (MNRE, July 2026). Generation is still coal-heavy, but the direction is clear. Where and how new data centres source their power will shape the footprint of India's digital economy.

The basics

So, what is a
data centre?

A building full of computers called servers. They store, process and send the data behind almost everything you do online, 24 hours a day.

Store
Photos, chats, bank records
Process
Payments, searches, AI answers
Deliver
Videos and pages to your phone
Think of it as a warehouse, but instead of goods, it holds the internet.

Why it matters for the planet

Data centres never sleep.
Neither does their
power demand.

Servers run 24×7, and so does the cooling that keeps them safe. All of it runs on electricity.

In 2024, data centres used about 1.5% of the world's electricity, and demand is set to more than double by 2030.

Source: IEA, Energy and AI (2025): global data centre electricity use, base case.

06 / 08
The hopeful part

India's power grid is
getting cleaner.

India has installed 300 GW+ of non-fossil power capacity, more than 60% of its 2030 target.

That's now over 54% of all installed capacity.

Honest note: capacity isn't the same as generation. Coal still supplies most of India's electricity today. The cleaner the grid gets, the cleaner every data centre on it becomes.
Source: MNRE, non-fossil installed capacity as of 31 July 2026 (300.5 GW).

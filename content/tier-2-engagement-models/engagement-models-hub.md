### H2: What every model includes

Whichever model you choose, you get the same baseline:

- **Senior-led delivery.** Engagements are led by engineers who have shipped and operated software at scale.
- **Secure-by-design engineering.** Security checks in the pipeline, not a pre-launch scramble.
- **Automated testing and CI/CD** from the first commit.
- **Observability.** Logs, metrics, traces and alerts on what we build.
- **Full handover.** Code, infrastructure-as-code, documentation and IP are yours, with no licence-back, no retained components and no lock-in.

### H2: How to choose

**Do you know exactly what you need built, and want someone else accountable for delivering it?**
→ [Outcome-based project](/engagement-models/outcome-based-projects)

**Do you have a product roadmap that will run for months, but no team (or not enough team) to deliver it?**
→ [Dedicated squad](/engagement-models/dedicated-squads)

**Do you have a team and a process, but need specific skills such as cloud, AI, security or mobile, quickly?**
→ [Staff augmentation](/engagement-models/staff-augmentation)

**Are you about to make a big technical decision and want an independent senior view first?**
→ [Advisory & architecture](/engagement-models/advisory-architecture)

**Not sure?** That's the most common answer. [Book a call](/contact) and we'll recommend one, including telling you if you don't need us.

### H2: Staff augmentation vs managed services vs outsourcing

These terms get used loosely, so here's how we use them:

- **Staff augmentation:** you add our engineers to your team. You direct the work and own delivery. We own their quality and replacement. [More →](/engagement-models/staff-augmentation)
- **Managed delivery (our squads and projects):** we own delivery of an outcome or a roadmap. You set priorities and accept the work.
- **Outsourcing:** a broad term for handing work to an external firm. Both of the above are forms of it. What matters is who is accountable for what.

If you're weighing up augmentation against a project team, read [staff augmentation vs project team](/compare/staff-augmentation-vs-project-team).

---

### H2: Moving between models

Engagements change shape, and that's fine. Common paths:

- **Advisory → project:** an architecture review turns into building the recommended solution.
- **Project → squad:** after launch, the project team stays on as a squad for the next roadmap.
- **Augmentation → squad:** two augmented engineers grow into a self-managing squad.
- **Squad → handover:** your in-house team takes over, with paired on-call and runbooks.

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Which model is cheapest?**
Per month, augmentation usually has the lowest commitment. But the cheapest model is the one that fits. Augmentation without strong internal technical leadership often costs more in the end.

**Can we start small?**
Yes. Many clients start with an advisory review or a single augmented engineer.

**Do all models follow your delivery process?**
Projects and squads run our [five-stage process](/process). Augmented engineers work inside yours, and bring the same habits with them.

**Who owns the IP?**
You do, in every model.

## Page copy

### H1: Outcome-based software projects: fixed scope, clear milestones, one accountable lead

You tell us the outcome. We agree a scope and cost envelope, then deliver against milestones you sign off. One delivery lead is accountable from kick-off to handover, and you pay for progress, not hours.

**[Start with discovery]**

---

### H2: When an outcome-based project is the right choice

- You know what you need: a product, a platform, a migration or a feature set.
- You want a predictable budget and a clear finish line.
- You'd rather hold one partner accountable than manage engineers yourself.

If your scope will keep shifting, a [dedicated squad](/engagement-models/dedicated-squads) is usually a better fit. If you just need extra hands, look at [staff augmentation](/engagement-models/staff-augmentation).

---

### H2: How it works

#### 1. Discovery (fixed price, 2 to 3 weeks)
We define goals, users, constraints and success metrics with you. You leave with a written scope, a risk register and a **cost envelope**: a cost and timeline range with every assumption written down. [More on discovery →](/process/discover)

#### 2. Milestone plan
We break the scope into milestones, each with clear acceptance criteria and a payment attached. Typical milestones: running slice, MVP, feature-complete, production launch, handover.

#### 3. Delivery
We run our [five-stage process](/process): architect, build in two-week increments, harden, then launch. You see working software every two weeks.

#### 4. Acceptance
At each milestone you review against the agreed criteria. You pay when the milestone is accepted.

#### 5. Handover
Code, infrastructure, documentation and runbooks transfer to you. Optional post-launch support under an SLA.

---

### H2: How we keep a fixed scope honest

Fixed-price projects go wrong in two ways: vendors pad the price to cover unknowns, or they cut corners to protect the margin. We avoid both:

- **Paid discovery first.** We price once we understand the problem, not after a sales call.
- **Assumptions in writing.** If an assumption turns out wrong, we both know exactly what changed.
- **A running slice early.** The riskiest technical assumptions get tested in weeks 3 to 6, not month five.
- **Visible change control.** New requests get a written impact on cost and timeline before anything changes. You decide.
- **No quality trade-off.** Tests, reviews, security checks and observability are in every milestone, not optional extras.

---

### H2: One accountable delivery lead

Every project has a single delivery lead, a senior engineer rather than a coordinator, who owns the plan, the risks and the outcome. You always know who to call, and they always know the answer.

### H2: Fixed scope vs time and materials

| | Fixed scope (outcome-based) | Time and materials |
|---|---|---|
| Budget certainty | High, within the envelope | Low |
| Flexibility to change | Through change control | High |
| Who carries delivery risk | Mostly us | Mostly you |
| Best for | Defined outcomes | Exploratory or evolving work |

We offer both. Time-and-materials work runs as a [dedicated squad](/engagement-models/dedicated-squads).

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Do you give a fixed price before discovery?**
We'll give you a rough order of magnitude on the first call, but we only commit to a cost envelope after discovery. Pricing before we understand the problem means padding or surprises.

**Is discovery a commitment to the full project?**
No. Discovery is a separate, fixed-price piece of work. The scope and envelope are yours to keep.

**What if we need to change the scope?**
You can. We'll show you the impact on cost and timeline in writing, and you decide.

**What if a milestone slips?**
The delivery lead flags risk as early as it appears. If a slip is on our side, it's on our cost. `[[VERIFY: commercial commitment]]`

**How are payments structured?**
Discovery is paid up front, and the build is paid per accepted milestone. `[[VERIFY]]`

### H2: Three ways we advise

#### Fractional CTO
A senior technical leader for a few days a month. Typical work: technology strategy, hiring your first engineers, vendor and build-vs-buy decisions, board and investor technical diligence, and keeping delivery honest.

#### Architecture & security review
A fixed-scope, independent review of your system. We look at architecture, cloud setup, security posture, reliability and cost, and give you a prioritised report: what's fine, what's risky, what to fix first.

#### Cloud & AI adoption roadmap
A practical plan for moving to the cloud, modernising a legacy system, or putting AI into production. That covers sequencing, costs, risks and what to build vs buy.

### H2: What is a fractional CTO, and do you need one?

A fractional CTO is an experienced technology leader who works with you part-time, often one to four days a month, instead of as a full-time hire. You get senior judgement at a fraction of the cost of a full-time CTO.

You probably need one if:

- You're a **non-technical founder** making technology decisions you can't fully evaluate.
- You're about to **spend significant budget** on a build or a vendor.
- You're **hiring your first engineers** and need someone to assess them.
- You're **preparing for investment** and need technical due diligence ready.
- Your team is **shipping slowly or breaking things**, and you can't tell why.

**How much does a fractional CTO cost?**
It depends on days per month and scope. `[[ADD our range]]` See our [rates guide](/blog/what-is-a-fractional-cto) for typical US and Australian market rates.

**Is a fractional CTO the same as a consultant?**
Not quite. A consultant usually delivers a defined piece of advice and leaves. A fractional CTO takes ongoing responsibility for technical direction, often sitting in leadership meetings and owning decisions.

**Can you review a system another firm built?**
Yes, and that's a common reason for a review. We'll be fair about what's good.

**Do we have to build with you afterwards?**
No. Many clients take the report to their own team.

**Do you sign NDAs?**
Yes, as standard.

**Do you work with startups?**
Yes. Funded startups are a large share of our fractional CTO work.

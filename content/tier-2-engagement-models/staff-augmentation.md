### H2: What is IT staff augmentation?

IT staff augmentation means adding external engineers to your own team for a defined period. You direct their work day to day. The provider handles hiring, vetting, payroll and replacement.

It's the right model when you already have a team and a delivery process, and what you're missing is specific skills or extra capacity. If you need someone else to own delivery end to end, a [dedicated squad](/engagement-models/dedicated-squads) or [outcome-based project](/engagement-models/outcome-based-projects) is usually a better fit. [More on the difference →](/blog/what-is-staff-augmentation)

### H2: How it works {#how}

1. **Brief (day 1 to 2).** A 30-minute call with a senior engineer to understand the role, the stack, your team and how you work.
2. **Matched profiles (within a week).** We put forward one to three engineers who fit, not a stack of CVs. `[[VERIFY]]`
3. **Your interview.** You interview and choose. Technical exercises welcome.
4. **Onboarding (about two weeks from brief).** The engineer joins your Slack, standups, repo and tooling.
5. **Monthly check-ins.** We review fit and delivery with you and the engineer every month.
6. **Handover.** At the end, documentation, pairing and a handover plan so nothing walks out the door.

Typically about two weeks from agreeing the brief. Some specialist roles take longer, and we'll tell you up front.

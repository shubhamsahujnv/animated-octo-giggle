### H2: What is a dedicated development team?

A dedicated development team is a group of engineers who work only on your product, for months rather than weeks. Unlike [staff augmentation](/engagement-models/staff-augmentation), where you manage individual engineers, a dedicated team comes with its own leadership and delivery process. You set priorities, and the team owns how they get delivered.

It's the right fit when:

- You have a roadmap that will run for six months or more.
- You don't have (or don't want to build yet) an in-house team big enough to deliver it.
- You want one accountable team, not a collection of contractors.

---

### H2: What's in a squad {#shapes}

Every squad is shaped around the roadmap. Three common shapes:

| Squad | Typical make-up | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Core squad** | Tech lead, 2 to 3 engineers, QA | A focused product or platform |
| **Product squad** | Product lead, tech lead, 3 to 5 engineers, designer, QA, DevOps | A new product from MVP to scale |
| **Platform squad** | Platform lead, cloud and DevOps engineers, SRE, security | Cloud foundations, migration, reliability |

AI-heavy roadmaps add AI/ML engineers and an evaluation specialist. Mobile roadmaps add iOS, Android or cross-platform engineers.

### H2: Why a Pixorama squad

- **Senior-led.** Every squad has a tech lead who has shipped and operated software at scale.
- **Accountable for outcomes.** The squad commits to sprint goals and reports on them, not just on hours logged.
- **Our delivery method, on your roadmap.** Discovery and architecture happen up front. After that, squads run the build → harden → operate loop of our [five-stage process](/process): two-week increments, automated tests, security in the pipeline, observability from day one.
- **Elastic.** Add a specialist for a quarter, or scale down after a launch, with 30 days' notice. `[[CONFIRM]]`
- **Stable.** The same senior engineers from month to month.
- **You own everything.** Code lives in your repo from the first commit.

### H2: How we set up a squad

1. **Discovery call.** Your roadmap, team, stack and timeline.
2. **Squad proposal (within a week).** Shape, roles, profiles and monthly cost.
3. **Meet the team.** You meet and approve the tech lead and key engineers.
4. **Kick-off (3 to 4 weeks from the call).** Onboarding, access, a first sprint goal.
5. **First increment (2 weeks later).** Working software in your environment.
6. **Monthly review.** Velocity, quality, cost and squad shape, reviewed together.

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**How fast can a squad start?**
Typically three to four weeks from the first call.

**Can we change the squad size?**
Yes. Scale up or down with `[[CONFIRM: 30]]` days' notice.

**Do we get the same people throughout?**
Yes, that's the point of a dedicated team. If someone has to leave, we overlap their replacement and hand over properly.

**Who sets priorities?**
You do. A Pixorama lead engineer runs delivery inside the squad, against the priorities you set. There's no account manager layer between you and the people doing the work.

**Can the squad hand over to our in-house team later?**
Yes. We plan for it with runbooks, documentation and paired on-call.

**Do you work with startups?**
Yes, with funded startups. A core squad is a common way to get from MVP to product–market fit without hiring a full team.

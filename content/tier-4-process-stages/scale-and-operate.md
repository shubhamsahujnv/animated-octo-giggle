## Page copy

### H1: Scale & operate: run it, improve it, then hand it over properly

Launch isn't the finish line. In the fifth of our [five stages](/process) we run the system in production, make it faster and cheaper, and transfer everything your team needs to own it. The goal is that you're never dependent on us.

---

### H2: Three ways to run after launch

| Option | What it means | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **We operate** | Pixorama runs the system under an SLA: monitoring, on-call, patching, incidents | Teams without in-house ops |
| **Shared** | We co-operate with your team, with paired on-call, while they ramp up | Teams building ops capability |
| **You operate** | Full handover at launch, with a support window | Teams with mature ops |

Systems we operate are covered by an agreed SLA. Across recent engagements, average SLA-backed uptime has been 99.98% (representative of recent work and confirmed per engagement). `[[CONFIRM: response times by severity]]`

---

### H2: Operate: what's included

- 24/7 monitoring and alerting `[[CONFIRM hours]]`
- Incident response with written post-incident reviews
- Security patching and dependency updates
- Backups and restore tests
- Monthly reporting on availability, incidents, performance and cost

---

### H2: Optimise: making it better after launch

Real users show you things testing can't. After launch we:

- **Tune performance** on the slowest, most-used paths
- **Cut cloud cost** by right-sizing, using reserved capacity and fixing architecture hotspots ([why cloud cost is an architecture problem](/blog/cloud-cost-is-an-architecture-problem))
- **Improve AI quality** with evaluation sets built from real usage, and reduce model cost per request
- **Scale ahead of demand** using real growth data

---

### H2: Handover: what you get

Handover isn't a zip file and a goodbye. It includes:

1. **Runbooks** for deployment, rollback, common incidents and routine tasks
2. **Architecture documentation** and the full set of [ADRs](/process/architect)
3. **Recorded walkthroughs** of each major component
4. **Paired on-call shifts**, where your engineers handle incidents with ours alongside
5. **Access transfer:** every credential, account, domain and secret, audited
6. **A defined support taper** after handover, so support steps down gradually rather than stopping `[[CONFIRM: length]]`

---

### H2: No lock-in, in writing

- All code and IP are yours, as they have been since the first commit.
- No licence-back and no retained components.
- No hosting lock-in. It runs in your cloud accounts.
- No proprietary frameworks you can't hire for.

---

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Can you support a system you didn't build?**
Yes, after an onboarding review. We'll usually start with an [architecture and security review](/engagement-models/advisory-architecture).

**How long does knowledge transfer take?**
Typically two to six weeks, depending on system size and your team's experience.

**Can we move from "we operate" to "you operate" later?**
Yes, at any time. The handover steps are the same.

**What does an SLA cost?**
It depends on system size, availability target and support hours. `[[ADD: indicative range]]`

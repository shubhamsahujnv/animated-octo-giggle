## LinkedIn caption

Every UPI payment, every reel, every cricket stream lands in a data centre.

India processed 24.07 billion UPI transactions in September 2026 (NPCI). Globally, data centres used ~1.5% of the world's electricity in 2024 (IEA). As India's data centre capacity grows, how that electricity is sourced becomes a material sustainability question.

Encouragingly, non-fossil sources now make up more than 54% of India's installed power capacity (MNRE, July 2026).

A 45-second explainer, the first in our series on data centres and the environment.

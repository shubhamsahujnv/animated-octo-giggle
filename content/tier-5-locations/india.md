### H2: How to choose a software development company in India

When you're comparing Indian vendors, ask:

1. **Who will actually do the work?** Ask to meet them before signing.
2. **Where does the code live?** It should be in your repository from day one.
3. **Can they show production work** they still operate, not just screenshots?
4. **How do they test and deploy?** Ask to see a pipeline.
5. **What's in the contract** on IP, notice periods and replacement?

We've written more on this in [how to choose a software development company](/blog/how-to-choose-software-development-company) and [the best software development companies in India, compared on evidence](/blog/best-software-development-companies-india).

### H2: Privacy and compliance

We build with Australian obligations in mind:

- **Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs):** data minimisation, access controls, cross-border disclosure considerations (APP 8)
- **Data residency:** Australian cloud regions where your data needs to stay onshore
- **Notifiable Data Breaches scheme:** logging and incident processes that support your obligations
- **Sector rules** where they apply, for example health records and financial services

We're engineers, not lawyers, so we'll work with your legal and compliance advisors and build to their requirements.

# Lynkk vs Avoma: coaching and revenue intelligence without the add-ons

Avoma is the closest thing on this list to what Lynkk is becoming: meeting notes, conversation intelligence, CRM updates and coaching, sold to mid-market revenue teams. If you are comparing the two, you have already worked out that Gong is too expensive and a plain notetaker is not enough.

The difference is how the bill is assembled. Avoma Startup is $19 a seat a month on annual billing. Then coaching, call scoring and scorecards are a $29 add-on. Deal risk, pipeline and forecasting are another $29 add-on. The seat you thought cost $19 is $48 or $77 by the time it does the job you bought it for.

Lynkk includes CUE live coaching and full account memory in the $19 plan. There are no add-ons.

## Where Avoma is the better pick

- **You want scheduling in the same tool.** Avoma's 1:1, group and round-robin scheduling is a real product, and replacing it means keeping a separate calendar tool. Lynkk does not do scheduling.
- **You need forecasting and deal-risk alerts.** The Revenue Intelligence add-on costs more, but it exists. Lynkk does not forecast.
- **You need a language outside Lynkk's 17.** Avoma covers 70+ for both transcription and translation. Check your list before switching.
- **You want HIPAA, SSO and a signed DPA now.** Avoma Enterprise covers those at $39 a seat annual. Lynkk's team controls are still arriving.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **Coaching is not an upsell.** The reason you are buying a tool like this is to make reps better. On Avoma that costs $29 a seat on top. On Lynkk it is in the $19 plan, and it happens during the call rather than in a review a week later.
- **You can actually try it.** Avoma has no permanent free plan — after 14 days you drop to a Viewer role that cannot record. Lynkk's free tier keeps working: five meetings a month, forever.
- **In-person conversations are in the record.** Avoma captures conferencing and dialer calls. The dinner where the deal turned is not in there. Lynkk records it from the Mac app and files it against the same account.
- **Memory across the account, on the plan you bought.** Avoma's global "Ask Avoma" — the one that searches across meetings — is Enterprise only, at $39 a seat annual with a 10-seat minimum. Lynkk's account memory with cited answers is in Pro.
- **No seat-count cliffs.** Avoma moves you between tiers at 25 and 100 seats. Lynkk's price is the same at 3 reps and 300.

## Switching from Avoma

**What moves over:** calendar, conferencing and CRM connections. Lynkk supports Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Gmail and Calendar, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your Avoma recordings, notes and scorecards. There is no import, so export before your access drops to Viewer.

**What to replace:** if you use Avoma's scheduling links, you will need a scheduling tool. That is a real cost of switching and worth pricing in.

## Questions

**Is Lynkk a good Avoma alternative?**
If you are paying for the Conversation Intelligence add-on, yes — you get coaching for less than the add-on alone costs. If you rely on Avoma's scheduling or forecasting, you will be giving something up.

**Does Avoma have a free plan?**
No. There is a 14-day trial of the Organization plan, after which accounts move to a Viewer role that can see past recordings but not make new ones.

**How much does Avoma really cost with coaching?**
$48 a seat a month on annual billing — $19 Startup plus the $29 Conversation Intelligence add-on. Monthly billing with the add-on is $64.

**Does Lynkk coach during the call?**
Yes. CUE is private to the rep and shows account context, objection responses, battlecards and commitment reminders while the call is happening.

**Can Lynkk record in-person meetings?**
Yes, from the Mac app. Avoma does not publish an in-person capability.

**Can I move my Avoma data into Lynkk?**
No. There is no import. Export what you need while you still have access.

# Lynkk vs Circleback: clean notes, or a memory that works the deal

Circleback has the best-looking meeting notes in the category and the automations to match. It records with or without a bot, it has mobile and Apple Watch apps for in-person, and the free plan is genuinely usable. If you are comparing it against Granola, you are choosing between two well-made tools that do the same job well.

Lynkk is answering a different question. Not "what happened in that meeting" but "what does this account know, what did we promise, and what happens next". Every conversation with a customer — calls, in-person, phone — goes into one memory per account, and that memory drafts the follow-up and updates the CRM.

If you need beautiful notes, Circleback is excellent. If you need the deal to move, keep reading.

## Where Circleback is the better pick

- **The notes are better.** This is the honest answer. Circleback's output is cleaner and more readable than almost anything else, Lynkk included, and if a tidy summary is the whole job, it wins.
- **Unlimited meetings on the free plan.** Circleback's free tier does not cap how many meetings you record, only how long the history sticks around. Lynkk's free tier caps you at five a month.
- **Apple Watch and mobile for in-person.** A watch tap is less conspicuous than opening a laptop, and Lynkk has neither a watch app nor a phone app.
- **Developer surface.** API, MCP and CLI access are on the free plan. If you want to build on top of your meeting data, Circleback makes it easy.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **Memory per account, not per meeting.** Circleback gives you an excellent record of one conversation. Lynkk links the discovery call, the follow-up, the in-person lunch and the renewal conversation, so you can ask what the economic buyer said about timeline and get an answer with the source attached.
- **It knows what was promised.** Commitments made on a call are tracked across the account until they happen, not summarised once and forgotten.
- **Coaching on the live call.** CUE puts account context, objection responses and battlecards in front of the rep while the call is running. Circleback does not do this.
- **Built for the revenue motion.** Follow-up drafts written from the account's whole history, CRM updates you approve, a manager view coming on Team. Circleback's automations are general-purpose; Lynkk's are aimed at a pipeline.
- **Hinglish and code-switching.** 17 languages with mid-sentence switching, which matters if your team sells in India.

## Switching from Circleback

**What moves over:** calendar and conferencing connections, in minutes. Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your Circleback notes and recordings. There is no import, so export anything you want to keep.

**What you will miss:** the Apple Watch capture, and the notes themselves for the first week. Give the account memory a fortnight before you judge the trade.

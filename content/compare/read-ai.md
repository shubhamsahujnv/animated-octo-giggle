# Lynkk vs Read AI: a Read AI alternative that only records what you point it at

Read AI is a meeting assistant that joins your calls automatically, transcribes them, and scores the meeting and the people in it on engagement, sentiment and talk time. It is broad, it is everywhere, and for a lot of teams the first thing they search for is how to get it out of their meetings.

Lynkk takes the opposite approach. It records the conversations you point it at, connects them into one memory per account, and turns that memory into follow-ups and CRM updates. No automatic calendar sweep, no participant scores, no surprise bot in a customer's call.

If you want meeting analytics across a whole org, Read AI is built for that. If you want a record of what your customers actually said and something that acts on it, keep reading.

## Where Read AI is the better pick

We would rather you pick the right tool than the one we sell.

- **You need phone apps.** Read AI ships iOS and Android. Lynkk does not have a phone app; it runs in the browser, on the Mac and in Chrome.
- **You are on Windows.** Read AI has a Windows desktop app. Lynkk's desktop app is Mac-only and needs Apple silicon.
- **You want meeting analytics for the whole company.** Read AI's engagement, sentiment and talk-time scoring across every meeting in an org is a genuine product category, and Lynkk does not compete in it.
- **You need HIPAA, SAML or SCIM today.** Read AI's Enterprise+ tier covers those. Lynkk's team controls are still arriving.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **Consent you can defend.** Lynkk records what you point it at. Nothing joins a customer call because it appeared on a calendar. If you have ever had to explain an uninvited notetaker to a prospect, that difference is the whole page.
- **Memory per account, not per meeting.** Read AI gives you a good report on one call. Lynkk links every call, email thread and in-person conversation with an account, so you can ask what the budget holder said about pricing in March and get an answer with the source attached.
- **It does the admin.** Follow-up drafts and CRM updates come out of the conversation automatically. You review, then send. That is the hour a rep gets back.
- **Live coaching on the call itself.** CUE shows account context, objection responses and battlecards while you are still talking, which is the moment they are worth something.
- **Hinglish and code-switching.** If your team sells in India, this is not a nice-to-have. Lynkk handles mid-sentence language switching; most notetakers pick one language per conversation.

## Switching from Read AI

**What moves over:** your calendar and conferencing connections, in minutes. Lynkk works with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams out of the box, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your Read AI history. Lynkk has no import, so past transcripts and reports stay in Read AI. Export anything you need to keep before you cancel.

**What to do first:** turn off Read AI's automatic calendar join before you run both in parallel, or your prospects will see two notetakers on the same call.

## Questions

**Is Lynkk a good Read AI alternative?**
If your reason for looking is that Read AI joins meetings you did not ask it to join, or that participant scoring makes your team uncomfortable, then yes. If you want cross-company meeting analytics or phone apps, Read AI is the better fit.

**Does Lynkk join my meetings automatically?**
No. You choose what gets recorded, every time. There is a bot if you want one, and a bot-free option through the Mac app or Chrome extension if you do not.

**Does Lynkk score my team on talk time?**
No. Lynkk's coaching is private and in the moment — context and objection help on the live call — not a scoreboard shown to managers after it.

**Does Lynkk train models on my conversations?**
No. Conversations are encrypted at rest and in transit and are not used for model training.

**Is Lynkk cheaper than Read AI?**
Not at the entry tier. Read AI Pro is $15 a month on annual billing against Lynkk Pro at $19. Lynkk includes account memory, follow-up drafting and live coaching at that price, which Read AI does not sell at any tier.

**Can I move my Read AI transcripts into Lynkk?**
No. There is no import. Export from Read AI first if you need the history.

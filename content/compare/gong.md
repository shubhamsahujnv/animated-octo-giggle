## Where Gong is the better pick

This is not a close call in every direction, and pretending otherwise would waste your time.

- **You need forecasting and pipeline inspection.** Gong Forecast is a real product with real adoption. Lynkk does not do forecasting.
- **You have a large org and a RevOps function.** Gong's trackers, scorecards and analytics across hundreds of reps are built for a team whose job is to run them. Lynkk is built for the rep and the manager.
- **You need enterprise procurement to say yes.** SOC 2 posture, legal review, SSO, data residency, a named CSM, a track record with companies your size. Gong clears that bar today.
- **Competitive and market intelligence at scale.** Gong's aggregate view across thousands of calls is something a $19 tool does not replicate.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **You can start today.** Free tier, self-serve, no procurement. A rep can prove the value this week instead of after a quarter of evaluation.
- **The price is the price.** $19 a seat, month to month. No platform fee, no credit pool that pauses your automations mid-quarter, no 10–20% surcharge for adding a seat mid-term, no 5–10% auto-renewal uplift.

## Switching from Gong

**What moves over:** your conferencing and calendar connections, and your CRM. Lynkk works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Gmail and Calendar, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your Gong call library and analytics history. There is no import. If your contract is ending, export what compliance requires before access lapses.

**A realistic path:** most teams do not rip Gong out mid-contract. Run Lynkk on the segment Gong does not cover well — the field team, the in-person motion, a new region — and make the platform decision at renewal with data instead of a deck.

## Questions

**Is Lynkk a real Gong alternative?**
For recording, account memory, follow-ups, CRM updates and coaching, yes. For forecasting and org-wide conversation analytics, no. If forecasting is the reason you are buying, buy Gong.

**How much does Gong actually cost?**
Gong does not publish prices. Third-party buyer data puts seats at roughly $1,200 to $2,400 a year with a platform fee on top, and the median contract around $55,000 a year.

**Does Gong have a free plan?**
No. There is no free tier and no self-serve checkout; trials run 14 to 30 days through sales.

**Can Lynkk record in-person meetings?**
Yes, from the Mac app, and they land on the same account timeline as your calls. Gong does not capture in-person conversations.

**Does Lynkk do forecasting?**
No. It gives you a CRM that reflects what customers actually said, which is the input to a forecast, but it does not produce one.

**What happens when I add a seat?**
On Lynkk, you add a seat at $19 and that is the whole transaction. On a Gong contract, mid-term seats are commonly 10 to 20% above your contracted rate.

# Lynkk vs Plaud: a Plaud alternative with no device to buy, charge or carry

Plaud sells hardware. The Note is a $159 card that sticks to your phone, the NotePin is a $159 wearable, and the Note Pro is $189. They record the room, you sync them, and a subscription turns the audio into notes. Limitless sells a similar pendant with a similar pitch.

Lynkk does the in-person recording from the Mac you are already carrying, then puts that conversation in the same place as your Zoom calls, your Teams calls and your phone calls — connected to the account it belongs to.

If you spend your week in rooms where a laptop would be rude, buy the device. If your in-person meetings are coffees, site visits and conference-room sessions where your laptop is already open, you do not need one.

## Where Plaud is the better pick

- **You record where a laptop does not belong.** Factory floors, hospital corridors, site walks, dinners. A pendant or a card on the back of your phone is genuinely better than opening a MacBook.
- **You want the recorder separate from your computer.** Some people want a device that does one thing and does not depend on an app being open.
- **You are not on a Mac.** Plaud's hardware does not care what laptop you own. Lynkk's desktop app is Mac-only and needs Apple silicon.
- **You record a lot of long-form audio.** Plaud's Unlimited plan at $29.99 a month has no minute cap, which is cheaper than most per-seat software if transcription volume is all you need.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **Nothing to buy, charge or forget.** The best recorder is the one you have on you. Plaud only works if you remembered it and charged it.
- **In-person and online in one place.** A Plaud recording lives in the Plaud app. Your Zoom calls live somewhere else. Lynkk puts the coffee meeting, the discovery call and the follow-up phone call on the same account timeline, so the context compounds instead of scattering.
- **It does something with the conversation.** Plaud gives you a summary. Lynkk drafts the follow-up email, updates the CRM and tracks whether the commitment made in the room actually happened.
- **No per-minute ceiling on the plan you will actually buy.** Plaud Pro gives you 1,200 transcription minutes a month for $17.99. Lynkk Pro is unlimited meetings at $19.
- **Total cost.** Plaud is $159 up front plus $99.99 a year minimum. Lynkk is $19 a month with a free tier and no hardware.

## What about Limitless?

Limitless sells a wearable pendant with the same core promise: always-on capture of the conversations around you. The trade-offs against Lynkk are the same as Plaud's — a device to carry and charge, recordings that live in their own app, and no CRM or follow-up layer. If you are weighing Plaud against Limitless, you are choosing between two pieces of hardware. Lynkk is the answer if you would rather not own one.

## Switching from Plaud

**What moves over:** nothing technical to migrate. Connect your calendar, install the Mac app, and you are recording.

**What does not:** your Plaud recordings and summaries. There is no import. Export what you need before you stop paying.

**What to keep:** the device, honestly. Plenty of people keep a Plaud for the rooms where a laptop is wrong and run Lynkk for everything else.

## Questions

**Is Lynkk a good Plaud alternative?**
If your in-person meetings happen with a laptop nearby, yes — and you skip the hardware cost. If you need to record where a laptop cannot go, Plaud is still the better tool.

**Can Lynkk record in-person conversations?**
Yes. The Mac app records in-person meetings and phone calls as well as Zoom, Meet and Teams, and files them all against the right account.

**Do I need to buy anything to use Lynkk?**
No. It runs on the Mac you have, in the browser, and as a Chrome extension.

**Is there a minute limit?**
Lynkk Pro is unlimited meetings, with a 2-hour cap per conversation. Plaud Pro caps you at 1,200 transcription minutes a month unless you pay for Unlimited.

**Does Lynkk work on Windows?**
Not as a desktop app. Lynkk runs in the browser and in Chrome on any machine, but the in-person recording app is Mac-only and requires Apple silicon.

**Which is cheaper?**
Lynkk, once you count the device. $228 a year against roughly $259 in Plaud's first year, and $159 of that is a thing you can lose.

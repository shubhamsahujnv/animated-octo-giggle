# Lynkk vs Notta: multilingual transcription without the translation add-on

Notta is a transcription tool with a strong multilingual story and a real following in Asia-Pacific. It records with or without a bot, runs on more platforms than most, and the base plan is cheap.

Then you read the pricing page. Monolingual translation is $6 a user a month on top. Bilingual transcription and translation is $9 on top. Notta Brain, the AI layer, is another $93.59 a year. The $8.17 headline is the floor, not the price.

Lynkk handles 17 languages plus Hinglish, with auto-detect and mid-sentence code-switching, on the plan you already bought. And then it does something with the conversation.

## Where Notta is the better pick

- **You need iOS or Android.** Notta has both. Lynkk does not have a phone app.
- **You are on Windows.** Notta ships a Windows desktop app; Lynkk's is Mac-only and needs Apple silicon.
- **You transcribe files more than you attend meetings.** Notta's upload, link transcription and screen recording make it a better general-purpose transcription tool.
- **You need a specific language Lynkk does not cover.** Notta's catalogue is broad. Check your language against Lynkk's 17 before you switch.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **The multilingual price is the price.** No translation add-on, no bilingual add-on, no separate AI subscription. Notta's $8.17 becomes $23.17 a user a month the moment you need both add-ons, which is more than Lynkk Pro.
- **Hinglish, properly.** Mid-sentence switching between Hindi and English is how a lot of Indian sales calls actually sound. Lynkk transcribes it as one conversation. Bilingual handling elsewhere is usually two languages running in parallel, not one sentence crossing between them.
- **A usable free tier.** Notta's free plan cuts you off at 3 minutes a conversation, which is not a meeting. Lynkk's free plan gives you five real meetings a month, two hours each.
- **It remembers the account, not the file.** Notta gives you a searchable transcript. Lynkk links every conversation with a customer and answers questions across all of them, with the source attached.
- **The work after the call.** Follow-up drafts, CRM updates, commitments tracked over time. Notta stops at the transcript.

## Switching from Notta

**What moves over:** your calendar and conferencing connections. Lynkk supports Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your Notta transcripts. There is no import, so export anything you want to keep first.

**Worth checking:** if you bought the Notta Memo device, Lynkk does not replace it directly — its in-person recording runs from the Mac app. See [Lynkk vs Plaud](/compare/plaud) for the hardware argument in full.

## Questions

**Is Lynkk a good Notta alternative?**
If you are paying for Notta's translation add-ons, almost certainly — Lynkk includes multilingual transcription in the base plan and costs less than Notta Pro with both add-ons. If you need phone apps or Windows, stay with Notta.

**How many languages does Lynkk support?**
17 plus Hinglish, with automatic detection and mid-sentence code-switching. Four are in beta.

**Does Lynkk charge extra for translation?**
No. There are no language add-ons.

**Is Notta's free plan usable?**
For short clips. The 3-minute cap per conversation means it will not transcribe a real meeting.

**Does Lynkk work without a bot?**
Yes. Record through the Mac app or Chrome extension and no bot joins the call. There is a bot option if you prefer one.

**Can I move Notta transcripts into Lynkk?**
No. Export them from Notta before you cancel.

## Structure

Every page follows the live `/compare/otter` template:

1. Breadcrumb — Home › Compare › vs X
2. H1 naming the differentiator, not just the two brand names
3. Short intro placing both tools, then an inline CTA
4. Capability table, around ten rows
5. **Where X is the better pick** — four honest concessions
6. **Where Lynkk is the better pick**
7. Pricing side by side
8. Switching from X — what moves, what does not
9. Six FAQs
10. Related pages and footer CTA

The honest-concession section is doing real conversion work on the live Otter
page and is kept on all eight.

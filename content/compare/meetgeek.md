# Lynkk vs MeetGeek: beyond the meeting summary

MeetGeek is a solid, conventional meeting assistant. It joins your calls or records bot-free from the Chrome extension or desktop app, transcribes in 100+ languages with automatic detection, and sends you a summary. The integration list is long and the free plan exists.

What it does not do is carry anything forward. Every meeting is its own artefact. Ask MeetGeek what the customer said about pricing across the last four calls and you are searching transcripts one at a time.

Lynkk is built the other way round. The unit is the account, not the meeting. Every conversation with a customer — online, in person, on the phone — goes into one memory, and that memory drafts the follow-up, updates the CRM and feeds the rep live on the next call.

## Where MeetGeek is the better pick

- **Language coverage.** 100+ languages against Lynkk's 17. If your team works in a language Lynkk does not cover, that settles it.
- **Phone apps.** MeetGeek has iOS and Android. Lynkk does not.
- **Integration breadth.** Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Attio, Notion, Asana, Confluence, Zapier, Make, n8n. If your stack is unusual, MeetGeek probably already connects to it.
- **Automation tooling.** Zapier, Make and n8n on the paid plans give you a lot of rope without writing code.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **The account is the unit, not the meeting.** MeetGeek files a summary per call. Lynkk links every conversation with a customer so the context compounds. Four calls in, the difference is obvious.
- **Cited answers.** Ask a question about an account and Lynkk answers with the source conversation attached, not a transcript you then have to read.
- **It does the work after the call.** Follow-up drafts written from the whole account history, CRM updates you approve before they write, commitments tracked until they happen.
- **Coaching during the call.** CUE shows account context, objection responses and battlecards while the call is live, privately to the rep.
- **In-person and phone on the same timeline.** MeetGeek's mobile app handles in-person. Lynkk puts in-person, phone and video calls on one account record, which is where the value is.
- **A free plan sized like a meeting.** MeetGeek's 3 hours a month is about two real calls, and the audio disappears after a month. Lynkk's five meetings a month at two hours each is a month of usable evaluation.

## Switching from MeetGeek

**What moves over:** calendar and conferencing connections. Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your MeetGeek transcripts and summaries. There is no import. On the free plan, note that audio is only kept a month, so export sooner rather than later.

**What to check first:** your language. If your team works outside Lynkk's 17 plus Hinglish, run a test call before you move.

## Questions

**Is Lynkk a good MeetGeek alternative?**
If you want the conversation record to do something — follow-ups, CRM updates, coaching — yes. If you mainly need broad-language transcription with a summary in your inbox, MeetGeek does that well and costs less.

**How many languages does each support?**
MeetGeek publishes 100+ with auto-detection. Lynkk supports 17 plus Hinglish, with auto-detect and mid-sentence code-switching, which is a different capability from raw count.

**Can both record without a bot?**
Yes. MeetGeek records bot-free through its Chrome extension or desktop app; Lynkk through the Mac app or Chrome extension. Both also offer a bot.

**Does Lynkk have a phone app?**
No. It runs in the browser, on the Mac and in Chrome. MeetGeek has iOS and Android.

**What does MeetGeek cost?**
Its pricing page shows Pro at $15.99 and $9.99, and Business at $27 and $17, without labelling which figure is monthly and which is annual. Check your quote.

**Can I import my MeetGeek history?**
No. Export it from MeetGeek before you cancel.

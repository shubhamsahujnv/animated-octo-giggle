# Lynkk vs Clari Copilot: revenue intelligence without the enterprise contract

Clari Copilot is the conversation intelligence product Clari built out of Wingman, which it acquired in 2022. It records calls, analyses them for deal risk, runs coaching scorecards and fires battlecards when a competitor gets mentioned. It is a good product, and it is the one buyers usually find right after deciding Gong is too expensive.

Two things have changed. Clari and Salesloft merged, closing in December 2025, and the combined company now owns two conversation intelligence products and two sales engagement products. Clari's own product pages now redirect to salesloft.com. The roadmap for which survives has not been published.

The other thing that has not changed is the shape of the purchase: no published price, buyer reports in the $120 to $160 a user a month range standalone, a 12-month minimum, and setup fees on top.

Lynkk does the recording, the account memory, the CRM updates and the coaching for $19 a seat, month to month, starting free.

## Where Clari Copilot is the better pick

- **You already run Clari or Salesloft.** If the revenue platform is in place, Copilot is the path of least resistance and the data lands where your RevOps team already works.
- **Deal-risk scoring matters to your forecast.** Copilot reads call language for risk signals and feeds the forecast. Lynkk does not do forecasting or risk scoring.
- **You need telephony coverage at depth.** Copilot captures across major dialers and telephony providers, which matters for a high-volume phone motion.
- **Enterprise procurement is already done.** Security review, MSA, SSO, a named team. Those are real costs you have already paid.

## Where Lynkk is the better pick

- **The price is public and small.** $19 a seat against a reported $120 to $160. For a ten-rep team that is roughly $2,280 a year against $14,400 to $19,200, before setup fees.
- **No 12-month minimum.** You can run Lynkk for a quarter, decide it is not for you, and stop. That is not an option on a Copilot contract.
- **No merger risk to absorb.** The combined Clari and Salesloft now own two conversation intelligence products. Someone has to decide which one lives. You do not have to be in the room for that decision.
- **In-person conversations count.** Copilot captures calls. Lynkk records the in-person meeting from the Mac app and puts it on the same account timeline.
- **Coaching while it still matters.** Copilot's scorecards are reviewed after the call. CUE shows the objection response during it.
- **You can start today.** Free tier, self-serve, no sales cycle. A rep can prove it this week.

## Switching from Clari Copilot

**What moves over:** calendar, conferencing and CRM connections. Lynkk works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Gmail and Calendar, plus in-person and phone.

**What does not:** your Copilot call library, scorecards and deal-risk history. There is no import. Export what compliance requires before access ends.

**Timing:** standalone Copilot runs on a 12-month minimum, so most teams evaluate at renewal. Running Lynkk alongside on a segment Copilot does not cover — field sales, in-person, a new region — gives you real data to renew or not on.

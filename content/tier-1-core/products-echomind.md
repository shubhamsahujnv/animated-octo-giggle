### H2: Journaling works. Keeping it up is the hard part.

Most people who start a journal stop within a few weeks. Not because it doesn't help, but because a blank page every night is hard, and it's difficult to see what you're getting out of it.

EchoMind is designed around those two problems:

- **You never face a blank page.** There's always a prompt ready, matched to what you've been writing about.
- **You can see what's changing.** Weekly summaries and patterns show you what your entries add up to.

### H2: What EchoMind does

#### Reflection prompts that fit your day
Choose from prompts for gratitude, stress, decisions, relationships, work and more, or let EchoMind suggest one based on your recent entries. Want a head start? See our [50 journal prompts for self-reflection](/blog/journal-prompts-for-self-reflection).

#### Pattern spotting
EchoMind notices recurring themes, such as the topics, people, times and moods that keep coming up, and shows them back to you gently. "You've mentioned sleep in four of your last six entries" is the kind of thing that's easy to miss on your own.

#### Weekly summaries
Every week you get a short, plain-language summary of what you wrote about, what seemed to lift you and what seemed to weigh on you.

### H2: What EchoMind is not

EchoMind is a journal, not a therapist and not a crisis service. It can help you reflect, but it can't diagnose, treat or replace professional support.

If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number.

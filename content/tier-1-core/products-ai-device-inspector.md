### H2: Manual inspection doesn't scale, and it isn't consistent

When devices are inspected by eye, two inspectors can grade the same device differently. That costs money in three places:

- **Disputes:** a buyer, insurer or customer challenges the grade and there's no evidence either way.
- **Margin:** devices get under-graded and sold too cheap, or over-graded and returned.
- **Throughput:** experienced inspectors are slow to train and hard to hire.

### H2: How it works

#### 1. Capture
A guided capture flow walks the operator through the photos and checks needed for each device type, so nothing gets skipped and the images are consistent.

#### 2. Grade
AI models assess cosmetic condition, such as scratches, cracks and dents, and flag likely defects. The operator confirms or overrides each finding, and every override is recorded.

#### 3. Report
A signed inspection report is generated with images, findings, the final grade and who approved it. The report can be shared with a buyer, customer or insurer as evidence.

#### 4. Audit
Every decision, whether by the model or a person, is logged with a timestamp and model version. If a grade is ever challenged, the full history is there.

### H1: We're a small, senior engineering firm that ships production software

Founded in 2022 and remote-first, Pixorama builds AI-powered software, cloud platforms and secure systems for enterprises and funded startups. We are deliberately small. The people who scope your project are the people who design it, and they stay on it until it's running.

**[Meet the team](/team)** **[Book a discovery call](/contact)**

---

### H2: Why we exist

A lot of software gets sold by senior people and built by junior ones. A lot of AI work gets demoed beautifully and never survives real users. And a lot of clients find out at the end that they don't really own what they paid for.

We started Pixorama to do the opposite:

- **Senior-led delivery.** Every engagement is led by engineers who have designed, shipped and operated software at scale.
- **Production, not prototypes.** We measure ourselves on what runs in production, not on what demos well.
- **You own everything.** All code and IP transfer to you at handover. No licence-back, no retained components, no hosting lock-in.

---

### H2: Six principles, every engagement

- **Senior-only teams.** Experienced architects and engineers, never juniors learning on your budget.
- **Outcomes, not hours.** We align on measurable business results and stand behind them.
- **Secure by default.** Security, privacy and compliance engineered in from day one.
- **Speed with certainty.** Production increments from week one, with controls that keep velocity safe.
- **No lock-in.** You own the code, infrastructure and documentation. We design for handover from the start.
- **A true partner.** Transparent communication, shared roadmaps and a relationship that compounds over time.

### H2: We build our own products too

We don't only build for clients. We build and run our own AI products, which means we live with the same production problems our clients do: on-call, costs, model drift, and support tickets.

- **[PXO AI](/products/pxo-ai):** a platform to design, deploy and operate AI voice and chat agents from one console. *In active development.*
- **[EchoMind](/products/echomind):** a private AI journal for iOS and Android with reflection prompts, pattern spotting and weekly summaries.
- **[AI Device Inspector](/products/ai-device-inspector):** AI-assisted device inspection with capture, grading and a signed report. *In build.*

What we learn running these goes straight back into client work.

### H2: How we work

Every engagement follows the same [five-stage process](/process): discover, architect, build, harden, scale and operate. You can engage us in four ways:

- [Outcome-based projects](/engagement-models/outcome-based-projects): fixed scope, milestone-based commercials
- [Dedicated squads](/engagement-models/dedicated-squads): a persistent team on your roadmap
- [Staff augmentation](/engagement-models/staff-augmentation): senior specialists, usually within two weeks
- [Advisory & architecture](/engagement-models/advisory-architecture): reviews, roadmaps and fractional CTO support

Every model includes secure-by-design engineering, automated testing, observability, and full handover of code and documentation.

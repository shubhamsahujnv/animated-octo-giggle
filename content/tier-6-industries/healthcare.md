### H2: Designed for consent, privacy and safety

#### Consent you can prove
Consent is captured, versioned and recorded per purpose. When a patient withdraws consent, the system knows what to stop doing.

#### Least data, least access
Collect only what's needed. Role-based and context-based access, with every access to a health record logged.

#### Clinical safety boundaries
Software that touches clinical decisions gets explicit boundaries: what it may suggest, what it must never do, and when a clinician must review. AI features get [guardrails and evaluation](/process/build) before they go anywhere near patients.

#### Security as standard
Encryption at rest and in transit, key management, segregated environments, audit logs and security review before launch. See how we [harden](/process/harden).

### H2: Regulatory awareness across our markets

We work with your compliance and clinical-safety leads and build the controls they specify. We're familiar with:

- **United States:** HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules, BAAs with vendors and sub-processors, and the ONC interoperability rules that push towards FHIR APIs
- **Australia:** Privacy Act and APPs, the My Health Records Act where relevant, and TGA rules if software qualifies as a medical device
- **India:** DPDP Act 2023, and ABDM standards for digital health
- **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD):** if your product might qualify, we'll flag it early, because it changes the whole delivery plan

### H2: Interoperability

Healthcare systems rarely stand alone. We build integrations using **HL7 v2** and **FHIR**, and connect to practice-management and EHR systems through their APIs or integration engines. Integrations are tested against realistic data and monitored in production, because a silent integration failure in healthcare is a safety issue.

1. **[Discovery](/process/discover):** users (patients, clinicians, admin), data flows, consent model and a regulatory map

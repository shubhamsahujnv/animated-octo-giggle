### H2: Buy, build or both?

Most logistics companies already run a TMS, WMS or fleet platform. The question isn't "custom or off-the-shelf?" It's which parts are commodity and which parts are how you win.

| Usually buy | Often worth building |
|---|---|
| Core accounting and payroll | Customer-facing tracking and portals |
| Standard WMS for common workflows | Routing and dispatch logic specific to your operation |
| Telematics hardware and base platform | Integrations that remove manual re-keying between systems |
| Generic TMS for standard freight | Pricing and quoting engines |
| | Operational dashboards that combine data from every system |

We wrote about this decision in detail in [logistics software: whether to buy a platform or build your own](/blog/logistics-software-development-companies-india).

### H2: The hard parts of logistics software

- **Real-time at scale.** Thousands of location updates a minute, event streams with Kafka, and ETAs that update quickly.
- **Offline-first mobile.** Drivers lose signal, and the app must keep working and sync cleanly.
- **Messy integrations.** Carriers, EDI, legacy systems and spreadsheets. Integration is usually most of the work.
- **Exceptions are the job.** Late, damaged, refused, re-routed. The software has to make exceptions visible and actionable, not just track the happy path.
- **Peak season.** Systems need [load testing](/process/harden) at peak, not average, volume.

### H2: AI that earns its place in logistics

AI is useful in logistics when it's tied to a decision or a cost:

- **"Where's my order?" calls** handled by an AI voice agent connected to your tracking data ([PXO AI customer service](/products/pxo-ai/use-cases/customer-service))
- **Document extraction** from PODs, invoices and customs forms
- **ETA prediction** that learns from your actual delivery history
- **Exception triage** that flags the shipments most likely to go wrong

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Should we replace our TMS?**
Usually not. More often the win is integrating it better and building the customer-facing and decision layers around it. Discovery will tell you.

**Can you integrate with our carriers?**
Yes, via API, EDI or, where needed, scheduled file exchange.

**Do your driver apps work offline?**
Yes. Offline-first is the default for field and driver apps.

**Can you work with our existing tech team?**
Yes, through [staff augmentation](/engagement-models/staff-augmentation) or a [dedicated squad](/engagement-models/dedicated-squads).

### H2: What actually makes up the cost of an AI voice agent

Whichever provider you choose, every minute of an AI voice call has the same building blocks. Knowing them makes quotes easier to compare.

| Component | What it is | What drives the cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Telephony** | The phone line and call minutes | Country, number type, inbound vs outbound |
| **Speech-to-text** | Turning the caller's speech into text | Minutes of audio, accuracy tier, language |
| **Language model (LLM)** | Understanding and deciding what to say | Model choice, length of conversation, amount of retrieved context |
| **Text-to-speech** | Turning the response into a natural voice | Voice quality tier, characters spoken |
| **Platform** | Orchestration, guardrails, integrations, analytics, storage | Features, agents, seats, retention |

### H2: What changes your monthly bill

- **Call volume and length.** Most bills track total minutes. A 2-minute booking call costs a fraction of a 10-minute support call.
- **Model choice.** Larger models cost more per minute. PXO AI can route simple turns to smaller, cheaper models. `[[CONFIRM]]`
- **Knowledge size.** More retrieved context per turn means more model tokens.
- **Outbound vs inbound.** Outbound telephony rates are usually different.
- **Recording retention.** Longer storage of audio costs more.

### H2: How to compare AI voice agent quotes

When you're comparing providers, ask each one:

1. Is the per-minute price **all-in**, or are speech, LLM and telephony billed separately?
2. Is there a **platform fee** on top?
3. How are **minutes counted**: per second, or rounded up to the minute?
4. What does a **transferred call** cost?
5. Are **failed or short calls** billed?
6. What do **integrations, analytics and recordings** cost?
7. Is there a **minimum commitment**?

We apply the same questions to ourselves, and to competitors, in our [AI voice agent comparison](/compare/best-ai-voice-agents).

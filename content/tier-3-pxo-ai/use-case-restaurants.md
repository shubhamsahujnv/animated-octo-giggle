### H2: Why restaurants miss calls

- **Peak hours are phone hours.** People ring to book or order at the exact moment you're busiest.
- **Front-of-house can't do both.** Every minute on the phone is a minute not seating, serving or settling bills.
- **Calls are repetitive.** Opening hours, parking, menu questions, "can I bring a cake?" and "is there a corkage fee?"
- **Missed calls go to the place down the road.** A takeaway customer who can't get through orders elsewhere.

---

### H2: What PXO AI handles for a restaurant

#### Reservations
Checks availability, takes party size, time, name and number, notes special requests (birthdays, high chairs, accessibility) and confirms by SMS. Handles changes and cancellations too. `[[VERIFY: booking-system integrations]]`

#### Takeaway and pickup orders
Reads from your current menu, takes the order item by item, handles modifiers ("no onions, extra sauce"), reads it back, gives a pickup time and sends it to the kitchen. `[[VERIFY: POS/ordering integrations]]`

#### Menu and dietary questions
Answers from your menu and allergen information. It's configured to be careful: for serious allergies it tells the caller to confirm with staff rather than guess.

#### Hours, location, parking and policies
Holiday hours, BYO and corkage, deposit and no-show policies, private dining and functions.

#### Overflow and after-hours
Turn it on for peak hours only, for after-hours only, or all the time. It's your call.

### H2: Allergens: where the agent stops

Food allergies are a safety issue, so PXO AI's guardrails are strict here:

- It answers only from the allergen information **you** provide.
- It never guesses whether a dish is "safe".
- For severe allergies, it recommends the guest speaks to staff and offers to pass on a note with the booking.

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Can the AI take phone orders accurately?**
It takes orders from your live menu, confirms each item and modifier, and reads the full order back before submitting it.

**What if a caller wants to speak to a person?**
They can ask at any time and the call transfers to your staff, or the agent takes a message if no one's free.

**Can I use it only during busy times?**
Yes. Schedule it for peak hours, after hours or all day.

**Does it handle large group or function enquiries?**
It collects the details and sends them to your events or functions manager. Big bookings usually need a human touch.

**Will it know today's specials?**
Yes, if you add them in the console. Changes take effect immediately.

### H2: Every missed call is a customer you didn't get

For a small business, the phone is often the main source of new work, and also the thing you're least able to answer.

- **You're busy doing the actual work.** Plumbers under a sink, stylists mid-cut, consultants in a meeting.
- **Callers don't leave voicemails.** They ring the next business on the list.
- **A full-time receptionist is a big cost** for a business that gets 20 to 40 calls a day.
- **Answering services read a script.** They can take a message, but they can't book the job or answer specifics.

### H2: Set up in an afternoon, not a project

You don't need a developer. `[[VERIFY onboarding flow]]`

1. **Tell it about your business:** services, prices, hours, service area, FAQs. Paste them in or upload a document.
2. **Connect your calendar** so it can book.
3. **Choose when it answers:** all the time, after hours only, or when you don't pick up within a few rings.
4. **Forward your number.** Keep the number your customers already know.
5. **Test it.** Call it yourself and fine-tune the answers.

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**What is an AI receptionist?**
An AI receptionist is an AI voice agent that answers your business phone. It talks to callers naturally, answers questions, books appointments and takes messages, then sends you the details. [Full definition →](/resources/glossary/ai-voice-agent)

**Will callers know it's AI?**
Yes, the agent introduces itself as an AI assistant. Most callers care far more about getting an answer than about who gives it.

**Can I keep my business number?**
Yes. Forward your existing number to PXO AI, all the time or only when you don't answer. `[[VERIFY]]`

**What if someone needs to talk to me urgently?**
Set rules, for example "transfer if it's an emergency or an existing customer", and the call comes to your mobile.

**How much does an AI receptionist cost?**
See [PXO AI pricing](/products/pxo-ai/pricing) for how it's charged. Use the [ROI calculator](/resources/ai-voice-agent-roi-calculator) to compare it with what missed calls cost you now.

**Is it hard to set up?**
No. If you can fill in a form and forward a phone number, you can set it up.

**Can it handle more than one call at a time?**
Yes. Unlike a person, it can answer several calls at once.

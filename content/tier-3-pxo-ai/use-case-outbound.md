### H2: Compliance first. Read this before you call anyone.

Automated and AI-voiced calls are regulated in every market we work in. Rules differ by country and change often, so **get legal advice for your use case.** Some of the key points:

- **United States:** the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) restricts calls using artificial or prerecorded voices. In February 2024 the FCC confirmed that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" under the TCPA, so you generally need the called party's **prior express consent** (and prior express written consent for telemarketing). Do Not Call rules, calling-hour limits and state laws also apply.
- **Australia:** the Do Not Call Register Act 2006 and the Telemarketing and Research Industry Standard set rules on who you can call, when, and what you must disclose.
- **India:** TRAI's commercial communication rules (TCCCPR) and the DND registry apply to promotional calls.

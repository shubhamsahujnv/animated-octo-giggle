### H1: An AI voice agent for customer service that resolves calls, not just deflects them

Most "AI" in customer service is a menu with a voice. PXO AI holds a real conversation: it understands the problem, looks up the order or account, takes the action, and hands over to a person with full context when it should. Your team handles the hard calls, and the agent handles the rest.

### H2: Why most voice automation frustrates customers

You've heard callers shout "agent!" at an IVR. The causes are predictable:

- **It can't understand open-ended speech**, so callers get pushed through menus.
- **It can't do anything**, because it has no access to orders, accounts or tickets.
- **It traps callers**, with no clean exit to a person.
- **Nobody can see why it failed**, so it never gets better.

Success is measured by resolution and satisfaction, not by how many callers the system kept away from agents.

### H2: What PXO AI resolves on its own

| Intent | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| **Order status and tracking** | Looks up the order, reads status and ETA, sends tracking by SMS |
| **Account and billing questions** | Verifies identity, explains charges, updates details within limits you set |
| **Bookings and changes** | Reschedules, cancels or upgrades in your system |
| **Returns and refunds** | Checks eligibility against policy, starts the return, issues labels |
| **How-to and troubleshooting** | Walks through steps from your knowledge base |
| **Outage and incident calls** | Recognises known incidents and gives the current status |
| **Everything else** | Collects the details, then hands over or creates a ticket |

### H2: Escalation that customers don't hate

Escalation is where most automation loses customers. PXO AI is designed around it:

- **Callers can always reach a person** by asking, and the agent never argues.
- **Rules you set** trigger hand-off by intent, value, sentiment, repeat contact or VIP status.
- **Warm transfer with context:** your agent gets a summary, the customer's identity, what's been tried and the transcript, before they say hello.
- **Fallback to callback or ticket** when queues are long.

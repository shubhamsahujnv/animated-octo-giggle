### H2: Guardrails come first in healthcare

An AI agent in a healthcare setting has to know what it must not do. PXO AI's guardrails are applied on every turn of the conversation, not just written into a prompt:

- **No clinical advice.** The agent is configured not to diagnose, triage symptoms or suggest treatment.
- **Emergency detection.** Phrases that suggest an emergency trigger your approved emergency script and an immediate hand-off.
- **Approved answers only.** The agent answers from your knowledge base using [retrieval](/blog/what-is-rag-in-ai). If the answer isn't there, it says so and takes a message.
- **Warm transfer.** When a person is needed, the call transfers with a summary, so the patient doesn't repeat themselves.
- **Full audit trail.** Every call has a transcript, the sources the agent used, and every action it took.

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Will patients know they're speaking to an AI?**
Yes. We recommend, and by default configure, the agent to say so at the start of the call. It's the right thing to do and in some places it's a legal requirement.

**Can the AI give medical advice?**
No. PXO AI is configured to avoid clinical advice and to escalate anything clinical or urgent to your staff.

**What happens in an emergency?**
The agent recognises emergency language, gives the emergency instructions you've approved (for example, call 911 or 000) and alerts your team.

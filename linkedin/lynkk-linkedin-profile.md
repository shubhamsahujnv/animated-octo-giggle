Based on an analysis of https://lynkk.ai (October 2026).

## Website analysis (summary)

- **What it is:** An AI meeting assistant and note taker for sales teams. It records calls, writes notes, and keeps every customer conversation in one searchable memory.
- **Core message:** Deals stall because context gets lost between calls ("The deal didn't go cold. The context did.").
- **Key features:** Records on Zoom, Google Meet and Teams (bot, Chrome extension, Mac app), plus in-person and WhatsApp/FaceTime calls. Groups memory by account and lets you ask questions across calls ("Ask Lynkk"). Drafts recap and follow-up emails. Sends tasks to Jira, answers to Slack and briefs to your CRM. Spotter is a live call assistant (early access). Supports 17 languages, including Hinglish.
- **Pricing:** Free (no card), Pro at $15/mo ($9.75/mo yearly), Enterprise on request.
- **Tone:** Confident, warm, short punchy lines, a little playful ("Upgrade when you're hooked").
- **Gaps to fix before going live:** The site lists no founders, location or company size, and its dashboard figures are marked "illustrative". The LinkedIn copy below makes no claims about these.

## 2. About / Overview (LinkedIn limit: 2,000 characters)

The deal didn't go cold. The context did.

Lynkk is the AI meeting assistant built for sales teams. It records your calls, writes the notes, and keeps every customer conversation in one connected, searchable memory. You walk into every meeting prepared and walk out with the follow-up already drafted.

What Lynkk does:

🎙️ Records anywhere: Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams through a meeting bot, Chrome extension or Mac app, plus in-person meetings and WhatsApp or FaceTime calls.

🧠 Remembers by account: every conversation is grouped by customer. Ask Lynkk anything across your calls, like "What did I promise this week?"

✉️ Drafts the follow-through: recap emails, follow-up messages and next steps, ready for you to review and send.

🔗 Keeps your tools in the loop: action items go to Jira, answers to Slack and call briefs to your CRM.

⚡ Helps you live: Spotter (early access) suggests responses while you're on the call.

🌍 Speaks your language: 17 languages, including Hinglish.

Built for sellers, account managers and sales leaders. It's just as useful for founders, consultants, financial advisors, real estate agents, lawyers, researchers and anyone whose work happens in conversations.

Your revenue lives in your conversations. Make every one count.

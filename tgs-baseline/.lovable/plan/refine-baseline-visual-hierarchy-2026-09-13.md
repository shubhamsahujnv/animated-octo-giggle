# Refine Baseline visual hierarchy

## Goal
Make Baseline calmer and more professional, with readable mobile colours and a simpler TGS-led header.

## Changes
- Replace the long header title with a larger TGS logo and “Baseline” beneath it.
- Remove the capex, arrow, eligibility-screener and Pilot labels from the header.
- Reduce excessive bold weights across headings, tabs, buttons, cards and labels while keeping clear hierarchy.
- Soften the heavy border and shadow treatment without changing page structure or functionality.
- Correct mobile text contrast on coloured surfaces and active navigation.

## Verification
- Check the public home page and a working calculator view on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm text remains readable, navigation still works and no horizontal overflow is introduced.

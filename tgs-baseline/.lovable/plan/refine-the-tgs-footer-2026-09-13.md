# Refine the TGS footer

## Goal
Give the public page a spacious, professional footer inspired by the reference without copying its marketplace content.

## Changes
- Replace the current row of small buttons with a structured multi-column footer.
- Lead with the larger TGS mark, a short TheGreensolve description, and “Baseline”.
- Group existing destinations under Platform, Company, and Contact so every link remains relevant and functional.
- Add a restrained lower bar for copyright and the existing carbon-credit disclaimer.
- Stack the columns cleanly on mobile with readable contrast, calmer typography, and comfortable spacing.

## Content safeguards
- Keep TheGreensolve as one word and TGS as the short form.
- Use only the confirmed email addresses: info@thegreensolve.com and media@thegreensolve.com.
- Do not invent legal pages, pricing, phone numbers, addresses, or partner names.

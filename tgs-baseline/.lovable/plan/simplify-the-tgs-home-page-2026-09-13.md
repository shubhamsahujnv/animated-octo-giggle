# Simplify the TGS home page

## Goal
Turn the current Home tab into a concise Carbon Intelligence Platform overview without changing the working calculators or other tabs.

## What will change
- Lead with **Carbon Intelligence Platform** and **Turn carbon data into business value**.
- Keep three direct actions: start the GHG inventory, open the Baseline calculator, and contact TGS.
- Add a compact proof strip using only defensible statements: India and US factor packs, ISO 14064 / GHG Protocol alignment, and TGS review before final submission.
- Present the active platform in four clear areas: carbon accounting, Baseline project screening, Net-Low employee engagement, and TGS review/reporting.
- Reduce the process to four steps: measure, compare, reduce, verify.
- Keep short About, partners, and Contact sections with the existing working form and TGS email addresses.
- Simplify the footer to useful navigation and correct the company name to TheGreensolve / TGS.

## Content safeguards
- Do not use unsupported claims from the older page such as “50+ manufacturers,” “SOC 2 aligned,” “blockchain-backed,” or fixed ROI percentages.
- Do not advertise AuditEase AI or Life Cycle Assessment as live modules unless they exist in this app.
- Keep regulatory and standards language precise and avoid implying that draft exports are submission-ready before TGS review.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the approved bold TGS palette and typography.
- Use fewer, stronger content blocks with clear color hierarchy and large action labels.
- Keep the page compact and mobile-friendly; no dense dashboard mockup or long feature catalogue.

## Verification
- Test Home navigation, all primary actions, anchored footer links, contact form submission, and mobile overflow.

Logo

The Y-mark and wordmark

The logo pairs a solar-to-red gradient Y with a red “OBYTECH” wordmark and the outlined “AUTOMATION” descriptor. Keep the three parts locked together. On dark backgrounds the red holds up well; on very busy imagery, place it on a navy or white plate.

  • Clear space: keep a margin equal to the height of the “O” in OBYTECH on every side.
  • Minimum size: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Below that, use the Y-mark alone.
  • Header size: 140–160 px wide, as on yobytech.com.
✕Don’t stretch
✕Don’t recolor
✕Don’t place on ember or red
✕Don’t add glows or effects
Color

Night navy, lit by ember

Navy carries the page. Ember orange is the single action color. Cyan marks live data and technology details, and stays small. Signal red and solar yellow belong to the logo; don’t spread them across the UI. Tap a swatch to copy it.

Voice & imagery

Speak like a senior engineer

Voice
  • Lead with the outcome: uptime, energy saved, water accounted for.
  • Name real equipment and units: pumps, SCADA, PLCs, kWh, m³/h.
  • Title Case for headings, sentence case for buttons and body.
  • Short sentences. No hype words without a number behind them.
Imagery
  • Real plants, substations, canals and factory floors.
  • Cool blue grade with thin light-trail overlays in cyan and gold.
  • Glass panels and dashboards over the scene, never cartoon icons.
  • Avoid generic stock robots and glowing brains.

Logo

The stethoscope DR

A red “DR” wrapped by a blue stethoscope, with “YOUR DIGITAL RECORD” beneath. It is a square lock-up, so it works for app icons and favicons. Below 64 px the descriptor becomes unreadable; drop it and use the DR mark alone.

  • Clear space: keep a margin equal to the width of the stethoscope’s chest-piece ring on every side.
  • Minimum size: 64 px with descriptor, 32 px mark-only (favicon).
  • Header size: 56–64 px tall.
✕Don’t squash
✕Don’t desaturate
✕Don’t place on brand blue
✕Don’t rotate
Color

Care blue, with a red pulse

Blue builds trust and carries navigation, links and primary forms. Red is the action and urgency color: “Book Appointment” and the “No more…” lines. Mint means done or healthy. White and pale blues give the clinic-clean ground. Tap a swatch to copy it.

Voice & imagery

Talk to a busy doctor

Voice
  • Start with the daily pain: calls, queues, paper, missed follow-ups.
  • Speak directly: “your clinic”, “your patients”.
  • Use the three-colour headline: statement in ink, “No more…” in red, outcome in blue.
  • Use Indian English and clinic terms: OPD, prescription, follow-up, ₹.
Imagery
  • Real Indian doctors, patients and front desks in bright, white clinics.
  • Product screens on laptop, tablet and phone, shown in use.
  • Flat 3D icons for modules (appointments, billing, EHR) on white.
  • Avoid surgery, blood, needles or distressed patients.

Fix list for the live sites

Gaps found while measuring yobytech.com and yodr.live on 2 November 2026, in the order they should be fixed.

High
yobytech.com has an empty meta description

Search results and link previews show no summary. Add one, for example: “YoByTech Automation builds AI-based IoT systems for water utilities, manufacturing, energy and agritech.”

High
Small white text on Pulse Red and Mint fails contrast

The 14 px “Book Appointment” button reaches 3.89:1. Darken the gradient start to #D62B2B (4.95:1) or raise the label to 18.7 px bold. For success badges, use #0E8F6C text on Mint Soft rather than white on Mint.

Medium
Company name spelled four ways

The site uses YoByTech, YoBytech, Yobytech and YOBYTECH across the title tag, alt text and footers. Standardise on “YoByTech” per the naming table.

Medium
YoDr loads four typefaces

Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, Poppins and Georgia all appear. Drop Poppins and Georgia; it removes a font request and makes headings consistent.

Medium
Two endorsers on YoDr

The title tag says “Powered by NXHT Healthcare & Research” and the footer says “Powered by YoByTech Automation”. Keep one endorsement line and move the legal entity to the copyright line.

Low
Theme colour doesn’t match the page

yobytech.com sets the browser theme colour to #FFFFFF on a navy site. Use #061225 so the mobile browser bar blends in.

Low
Logo files are large raster images

The YoDr logo ships as a 1080 px PNG (about 300 KB) and the YoByTech logo as a 1708 px WebP. Commission SVG versions of both; they’ll be sharper and much lighter.

Low
Price format

Pricing reads “₹ 3999”. Write “₹3,999 / year” with Indian digit grouping and the billing period.

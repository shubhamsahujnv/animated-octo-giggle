# Pixorama Service Page Content

23 October 2026

Five service pages, one per real capability, written to the Service Page Strategy for Leads (v1.1).
None is a ranking bet: Pixorama has 31 referring domains against a Gate 2 threshold of 62, and the
weakest competitor in every SERP tested has 168 to 711, so zero keywords cleared Gates 1 to 4. These
pages exist to convert the traffic that already arrives, to give AI assistants something per-service
to cite, and to be aged and ready for when authority follows.

## The five pages

Slugs use the wording buyers search; nav, footer and CTAs keep Pixorama's own capability names.
Three slugs changed from the capability names because those names carry almost no search demand.

Volumes are US monthly, from Ubersuggest, 23 October 2026. A sixth page, `/services`, lists these
five and replaces the current orphan route that serves homepage content under a homepage canonical.

Every page carries the same structure: an answer-ready opening of 40 to 60 words, a scope table,
timelines, a five-step process, the four engagement models, proof, what drives cost, who it is and
is not for, an FAQ, internal links, and a form that attributes the lead to its page.

**Every H2 carries the keyword or a close variant**, per the strategy's keyword-placement rule, and
at least half of each page's FAQ questions name the service the way a buyer would ask about it. The
first draft used generic headings ("What's included", "Who it's for") that carried no keyword at
all; the build now rejects a page whose headings go generic again.

**Do we own the AI system you build?** Completely: code, infrastructure, prompts, evaluation sets and

4. **Harden offline, payments and deep links** The parts that fail in the field: sync on a bad connection, store billing, link routing.
5. **Submit, release and hand over** Store listings, review handling, and the signing assets and code transferred to you.

## Before launch

These block publishing, regardless of how good the copy is.

- [ ] **Wire the enquiry endpoint.** It returns 501 in production on purpose, so a half-wired form
      cannot look successful to a real visitor. Point it at whatever already handles the site's
      contact submissions rather than adding a second path.
- [ ] **Confirm the GA4 property is Pixorama's own** before trusting any event.
- [ ] **Track WhatsApp enquiries.** The site routes enquiries through WhatsApp and email as well as
      the form. Untracked, those leads cannot be attributed and these pages will read as failing when
      they are not.
- [ ] **Add the five routes plus `/services` to the sitemap.**
- [ ] **Point the footer's Capabilities column at the five pages.** All five entries currently link to
      the same `#capabilities` anchor.
- [ ] **Point the nav's Services item at `/services`** instead of `/#capabilities`.
- [ ] **Move the five `Service` schema entries off the homepage `OfferCatalog`** onto the pages they
      describe. Leave `Organization` and `WebSite` on the homepage; the service pages reference
      `Organization` by id.
- [ ] **Remove the migrated FAQ questions from the homepage.** Nine were split across these pages by
      topic; duplicate `FAQPage` markup competes with itself.
- [ ] **Decide what to do about `/about` and `/contact`**: orphan routes serving homepage content
      under a homepage canonical. Build them out or remove them.

### How these pages should be judged

Not on rankings this quarter, and not on page count. Qualified leads per page, split by source, at
**day 120**. Do not prune before **day 180**, the bottom authority band in the strategy's timeline.

profile with reviews from the five existing clients, named case studies with numbers, named

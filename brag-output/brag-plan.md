# TheGreensolve: Diwali 2026 reel, plan (v3: editorial illustration)

Built with the **/brag-slim** skill (latent-spaces/brag) and styled with **thegreensolve.com**'s design system.

## Brief answers
- **What it is:** A Diwali greeting from TheGreensolve (TGS), the carbon intelligence company behind *Baseline*. It is told as a short animated story with an environmental message.
- **Who it's for:** TGS's audience of manufacturers, sustainability teams and partners, plus anyone scrolling Instagram or LinkedIn during Diwali week.
- **Style:** Premium editorial illustration with no characters or faces. A refined clay diya, a line-art wireframe globe with an orbit (a nod to carbon intelligence), a botanical marigold sprig, a geometric rangoli, and fine hanging lights. All of it is in TGS's palette and type.
- **Hook:** A diya ignites in the dark and light ripples outward. Then: *"Light diyas, not emissions."*
- **Story:** The diya lights. Its light clears the smoke around the globe. A marigold grows. Eight diyas connect into a rangoli. The outro shows the TGS logo and "Happy Diwali".
- **Tone:** `polished` and calm, matching the site's practical voice.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Grounding
- No invented numbers, stats or testimonials. The message is general and positive: less smoke, clay diyas, plants.
- No personal data. Only the public domain `thegreensolve.com` appears on screen.

## Format
Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, **20.0 s**. Music at **96 BPM**, so 1 beat = 0.625 s and the video is 32 beats. Every cut lands on a beat.

## Sound
Synthesized in D at 96 BPM, on one beat grid from frame 0:
- A tanpura-style drone, santoor-style plucks, soft tabla and kick, and brushed shakers.
- Bells and shimmers are in D major pentatonic: the ignite, the globe drawing on, the arpeggio as the smoke clears, the rising pluck as the stem grows, the bloom chord, one bell per diya in the ring, and the logo chord.
- Soft whooshes mark the transitions.

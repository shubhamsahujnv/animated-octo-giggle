# TheGreensolve: Diwali 2026 reel, plan

Built with the **/brag-slim** skill (latent-spaces/brag) and styled with **thegreensolve.com**'s design system.

## Brief answers
- **What it is:** A Diwali greeting from TheGreensolve (TGS), the carbon intelligence company behind *Baseline*, with practical tips for a lower-impact Diwali.
- **Who it's for:** TGS's audience of manufacturers, sustainability teams and partners, plus anyone scrolling Instagram or LinkedIn during Diwali week.
- **What sets it apart:** It doesn't use fireworks clip-art. It uses TGS's own visual language: teal hero cards, lime pills, mono `01 · LABEL` steps, outline cards with hard shadows, and "connected actions" framing. The festival becomes a short checklist in the style of a TGS product page.
- **Hook:** A single clay diya ignites in the dark, and the line *"Light diyas, not emissions."* appears.
- **Visual hook:** The flame's glow is the brand lime `#E2FF4D`.
- **Real UI to show:** The site's "One platform. Four connected actions." card grid, reworked as "Four connected actions for Diwali." The `Measure → Compare → Reduce → Verify` arrow flow also appears.
- **Tone:** `polished` with warmth. Calm and practical, no hype, matching the site's plain B2B voice.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Grounding
- Brand copy reused from the site: "connected actions", the `01 · LABEL` step format, and the arrow flow (Measure → Compare → Reduce → Verify).
- The eco tips are general Diwali guidance. There are **no invented numbers, stats or testimonials**.
- No personal data. Only the public domain `thegreensolve.com` appears on screen.

## Format
Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, **20.0 s**. Music at **96 BPM**, so 1 beat = 0.625 s and the video is 32 beats. Every cut lands on a beat.

## Sound
Composed in D, at 96 BPM, on one beat grid from frame 0:
- A tanpura-style drone on D–A, with a slow swell.
- A soft kick on beats from scene 2, with brushed shakers on 8ths.
- A plucked santoor-like melody in D major pentatonic (D E F# A B).
- Each SFX is a bell tuned to the scale: the flame ignite (A), each card (rising D, E, F#, A), and each diya in the row (an ascending pentatonic run).
- The SFX sit under the music, and the master ends with a fade-out.

## Poster
The settled outro frame at about 19.2 s: logo, "Happy Diwali" and the URL pill. It is baked in as frame 0.

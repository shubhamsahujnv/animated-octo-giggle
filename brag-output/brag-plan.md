# TheGreensolve: Diwali 2026 reel, plan (v2: cartoon story)

Built with the **/brag-slim** skill (latent-spaces/brag) and styled with **thegreensolve.com**'s design system.

## Brief answers
- **What it is:** A Diwali greeting from TheGreensolve (TGS), the carbon intelligence company behind *Baseline*. It is told as a short cartoon story with an environmental message.
- **Who it's for:** TGS's audience of manufacturers, sustainability teams and partners, plus anyone scrolling Instagram or LinkedIn during Diwali week.
- **What sets it apart:** Three original cartoon characters carry the message, so there's no checklist. A **diya buddy** (a clay diya with a face), **Earth**, and a **plant pot** that sprouts a marigold. All of it is drawn in TGS's palette and type.
- **Hook:** A sleepy diya buddy wakes up, its flame pops on, and it hops. Then: *"Light diyas, not emissions."*
- **Story:** Earth is stuck in cracker smoke, the diya's light blows the smoke away, a plant blooms, the diyas dance, and the trio waves "Happy Diwali".
- **Tone:** Warm, playful and cartoony, while keeping the brand's calm voice.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Grounding
- No invented numbers, stats or testimonials. The message is general and positive: less smoke, clay diyas, plants.
- No personal data. Only the public domain `thegreensolve.com` appears on screen.

## Format
Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, **20.0 s**. Music at **96 BPM**, so 1 beat = 0.625 s and the video is 32 beats. Every cut lands on a beat.

## Sound
Synthesized in D at 96 BPM, on one beat grid from frame 0:
- A tanpura-style drone, santoor-style plucks, soft tabla and kick, and brushed shakers.
- The foley is cartoony but tuned to the key: boings on hops, pops as the smoke puffs in, soft coughs, a thud when the pot lands, and a rising pluck run as the stem grows.
- Bells are in D major pentatonic: on the ignite, the sparkle arpeggio, the marigold bloom, each diya in the chorus, and the logo.

## Poster
The settled outro frame at about 19.2 s: logo, "Happy Diwali" and the URL pill. It is baked in as frame 0.

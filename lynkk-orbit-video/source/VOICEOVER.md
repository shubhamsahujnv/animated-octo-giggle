# Voiceover for The Orbit (record and lay over the finished film)

Calm, warm, unhurried. Pause at each "..." so the line meets the ring locking in.

| Starts at | Line | What is on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0 s | At the centre of every team... | the sphere lights and pulses (1.0 s) |
| 2.2 s | ...are its conversations. | Remi draws the meeting-app ring |
| 4.2 s | Lynkk captures each one. | the ring locks, "Records every call." |
| 6.0 s | Connects them to your calendar, your Slack... | Calendar and Slack ring |
| 8.6 s | ...your CRM... | CRM ring, lock at 9.5 s |
| 10.0 s | ...and turns them into memory. | the drop, "Every call. One memory." |
| 12.6 s | Lynkk. | the sphere locks as the logo at 13.5 s (say "Lynkk" right on it if you can) |
| 13.6 s | Meeting memory for teams. | tagline, then Start for free at 14.0 s |

Mix the voice about 6 dB above the music bed; duck the music by 3 to 4 dB under the lines.

# Style: Lynkk for Mac launch film

**Direction.** A launch film for a Mac app, made the way Apple shows a product:
the Mac itself is the stage. The menu bar is the spine: every panel comes out of
the Lynkk mark in the menu bar and folds back into it. We never cut away from the
desktop once we are on it.

**Motion.** Linear's grammar. Text slides out from behind invisible masks, never
scales or blurs in. Sharp acceleration, long deceleration
(`cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, the site's own easing), no bounce. Lists stagger in
the way the app's lists do. Long holds: every line stays settled for at least
0.3 s per word.

**Rhythm.** 120 BPM, one beat = 0.5 s, one bar = 2 s. Every cut, keypress and
text entrance lands on the grid in `beats.json`. Sound is music plus real
sounding foley: key switches, clicks, a sub thud on the big moments.

**Hero objects.** The `fn` keycap, the `⌘` keycaps, the menu-bar mark. Shot macro,
like hardware.

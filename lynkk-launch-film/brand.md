# Brand: Lynkk (for the Mac launch film)

Source of truth: `../lynkk-post-kit/` (Grid design system, TRUTHS.md, COPY.md),
lynkk.ai and lynkk.ai/download. Every claim in the film is in
`../lynkk-post-kit/docs/TRUTHS.md`.

## Colour (from `kit/grid/tokens.css` and the live site)
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Night band | `#111111` |
| Ink | `#FFFFFF` at 100% to read, 50 to 60% to skim (two inks) |
| Surfaces in mocks | `#1E1E21`, `#262629`, white glass bodies |
| Violet, label only | `#7063BE`, soft `#B4ABE0`, deep `#231F3A` |
| Live, recording only | `#E5484D` |

## Type
- Headlines: **Schibsted Grotesk** 500, the lynkk.ai hero face (OFL, bundled).
- UI mocks and small lines: **Inter** 400/500 (from the kit).
- No bold, no light. Tight tracking at display sizes.

## Assets
- `assets/mark.svg`: the striped sphere, 13 stripes, from the kit.
- `assets/plates/night.jpg`, `assets/plates/ember.jpg`: from the kit.

## Rules carried from the kit
No gradients, no shadows, no emoji, no em dashes, no competitor or third-party
brand names, no prices. Violet only as a label. Red only for recording.
Sample data comes from lynkk.ai and lynkk.ai/download mocks (Corvane Logistics,
Dana Ruiz, Marco Bell, Priya Nair, Sam Ortiz, the forty seat pilot).
